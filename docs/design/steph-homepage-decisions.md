# Steph homepage implementation decisions

- The hero guide label is **FREE HOMEBUYER GUIDE**, rather than the reference’s **FREE PCS GUIDE**. The available delivered document is the existing First-Time Homebuyer Guide; no national PCS guide has been provided. The reference’s guide art, placement, and capture interaction are retained.
- Success-story quotations and names come from the existing Google Business Profile review data. The extracted reference family photo has its own “VeteranPCS family photo” caption. No review, identity, military affiliation, or bonus amount is assigned to that family from the reference placeholder text.
- The impact ribbon displays numerical cash-back and charity totals only when the shared impact provider reports verified data. Unavailable metrics use nonnumeric community copy.
- Phone layouts omit the inline bonus calculator, with access retained in Browse Resources at `/pcs-resources#move-in-bonus`. Recruitment, internship, and newsletter sections follow the final lender section.
- Homepage changes use a new composition and scoped styles. Existing shared homepage components, including Spanish consumers of the legacy hero and map, are preserved.
