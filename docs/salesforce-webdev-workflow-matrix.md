# WebDev native workflow test matrix

The ten website submissions, native five-minute welcome with inbox and email-log correlation, and the real SMS queue worker have separate receipts. The scenarios below exercise the remaining Salesforce entry points. The expected results come from the November 25 retrieved metadata and the deployed capture overlay. A test result for one entry point covers that entry point only. [Environment evidence](salesforce-webdev-environment.md)

- Reconfirm the exact WebDev org and sandbox flag, the active Flow versions, existing jobs, and the next fire times. [CronJobDetail](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-cronjobdetail.html) · [CronTrigger](https://developer.salesforce.com/docs/platform/object-reference/references/standard-objects/object-crontrigger.html)
- Use new marked synthetic records and record their IDs and effective fields after automation. Bind any related Account, Contact, Agent, Lender or owner to a verified WebDev fixture. Use the approved `tech@veteranpcs.com` email and reserved fictional capture-only phone numbers. Keep **System email only** for capture and trace runs. If a separate positive mailbox receipt is required, use the bounded native email gate and restore deliverability afterward. [Native email gate](../salesforce/scripts/webdev-native-email-gate.md)
- Reserve an idle window and a distinct capture label. Verify pre-run counts and any unrelated eligible rows before invoking a batch or relying on a scheduled run. Capture evidence contains no source record ID, so correlate by the isolated run label, action, item count and timestamp. Record exact fixture field readback, scheduled and actual timestamps, relevant Flow or Apex action names, and provider receipt counts. Keep raw logs and recipient data private. [Capture evidence and run label](../salesforce/outbound-capture/README.md)
- Native Flow email alerts and direct Send Email actions use the recipient overlay. Capture-only runs can verify their branch and any record changes through Flow traces, but only a bounded delivery window can verify actual mailbox arrival. Apex `ReminderEmail`, `SenderRegistry` and `Sms` captures demonstrate their terminal guard and do not prove mail or SMS delivery.
