Most people think of Texas as Army and Air Force country. But the naval bases in Texas play a big role, too. This is where a large share of Navy and Marine Corps pilots learn to fly. Texas has three Navy installations: two training air stations on the Gulf Coast and one joint reserve base in Fort Worth. This guide covers what each base does, which town it is near, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like nearby.

| Installation | Nearest city | Best known for |

| NAS Corpus Christi | Corpus Christi | Navy flight training headquarters and the Army's helicopter depot |
| NAS Kingsville | Kingsville | Advanced jet training in the T-45 Goshawk |

NAS stands for Naval Air Station. JRB stands for Joint Reserve Base.

## NAS Corpus Christi

Naval Air Station Corpus Christi sits on the water. Corpus Christi Bay, Oso Bay and the Laguna Madre surround it on three sides, per the [NAS Corpus Christi installation page](https://cnrse.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-Corpus-Christi/). Navy pilot training started here in 1941.

Today, Navy, Marine Corps, Coast Guard and foreign student pilots earn their wings here. The flying is done by Training Air Wing Four (TRAWING 4). Per the [TRAWING 4 page](https://www.cnatra.navy.mil/tw4/), the wing has four squadrons:

- VT-27 and VT-28 teach basic flight training.
- VT-31 and VT-35 teach intermediate and advanced multi-engine flying.
- The wing has about 800 officer and enlisted personnel.
- It runs more than 180 aircraft and simulators.

NAS Corpus Christi is also home to the Chief of Naval Air Training (CNATRA), the command that runs all Navy pilot training. Another big tenant is the Corpus Christi Army Depot. The Navy calls it the main depot repair site for the Defense Department's helicopters.

For neighborhoods, schools and commutes, read our [PCS guide to NAS Corpus Christi](/blog/pcs-to-nas-corpus-christi-2026-guide).

## NAS Kingsville

NAS Kingsville is south of Corpus Christi. It is a jet school. Training Air Wing Two (TRAWING 2) turns student pilots into Navy and Marine Corps jet and strike pilots, per the [TRAWING 2 page](https://cnrse.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-Kingsville/About/Tenant-Commands/Training-Air-Wing-Two/). Here are the key numbers from that page:

- About 200 flight students report each year.
- They train in two squadrons, VT-21 and VT-22.
- Training lasts 11 to 13 months and ends with the student earning "Wings of Gold."
- The wing flies 85 T-45 Goshawk jets.
- TRAWING 2 trains half of the Navy and Marine Corps jet and strike pilots each year.

Kingsville is a small town, so many families compare it with Corpus Christi before they choose where to live. Both fall in the same BAH rate area, TX275, so your housing pay is the same either way.

## NAS JRB Fort Worth

NAS Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth has a long history. The site was known as Carswell Air Force Base from 1948 to 1993, per the [NAS JRB Fort Worth history page](https://cnrse.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-JRB-Fort-Worth/About/History/). Carswell was picked for closure in 1991 and closed in September 1993. In October 1994, it reopened as a Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base.

Today it is a true joint base. The [base's tenant command list](https://cnrse.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/NAS-JRB-Fort-Worth/About/Tenant-Commands/) includes:

- Navy units such as Fleet Logistics Support Squadron 59 (VR-59) and Commander Fleet Logistics Support Wing.
- The Air Force Reserve's 301st Fighter Wing and 10th Air Force headquarters.
- The Texas Air National Guard's 136th Airlift Wing.
- Army Reserve and Army aviation units.

Because this is a reserve base, many of the people you meet here are reservists or Guard members. Lockheed Martin's plant sits right next door, so you will see a lot of defense work in the area.

## What BAH Covers Near Texas Naval Bases

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off base. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code, and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Corpus Christi and Kingsville | TX275 | $1,788 / $1,413 | $2,310 / $1,734 | $2,475 / $2,061 |
| Fort Worth | TX356 | $2,118 / $1,656 | $2,445 / $1,941 | $2,460 / $2,310 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 in the Corpus Christi and Fort Worth rate areas](/images/blog/naval-bases-in-texas/texas-navy-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near Texas's Navy bases. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- An E-5 with dependents gets $330 more a month in Fort Worth than in Corpus Christi.
- At O-3, the gap almost disappears. Corpus Christi pays $15 more with dependents.
- In Corpus Christi, an E-5 with dependents gets $375 more a month than an E-5 without dependents.

BAH is meant to cover typical rent and utilities, not your dream home. ## Buying a Home Near Texas Naval Bases

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the home's appraised value. You also do not need private mortgage insurance. Our guide to [Texas VA loan benefits](/blog/texas-va-loan-benefits-for-military-homebuyers) covers state programs that stack on top.

**Know the Texas tax picture.** The state says plainly that "Texas does not have a state personal income tax," per the [Texas.gov taxes page](https://www.texas.gov/business-taxes/taxes). Property taxes do the heavy lifting instead, so always ask for the tax bill on any home you like. Disabled veterans may get big breaks. See our guide to [Texas veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/texas-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

**Think about the coast.** Corpus Christi and Kingsville are on the Gulf Coast. Ask your agent about flood zones and windstorm insurance before you make an offer. These costs can change your monthly payment a lot.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Navy bases are in Texas?

Texas has three Navy installations: NAS Corpus Christi, NAS Kingsville and NAS Joint Reserve Base Fort Worth.

### What is the main Navy base in Texas?

NAS Corpus Christi is the biggest Navy hub in Texas. It is home to the Chief of Naval Air Training, Training Air Wing Four and the Corpus Christi Army Depot.

### Does the Navy train pilots in Texas?

Yes. Training Air Wing Four at NAS Corpus Christi teaches basic and multi-engine flying. Training Air Wing Two at NAS Kingsville trains half of the Navy and Marine Corps jet and strike pilots each year.

### Do Corpus Christi and Kingsville have the same BAH?

Yes. Both fall in rate area TX275. An E-5 with dependents gets $1,788 a month there in 2026.

### Is NAS JRB Fort Worth an active duty base?

It is a joint reserve base. Most of its tenant units are Navy Reserve, Air Force Reserve, Air National Guard and Army Reserve commands.
