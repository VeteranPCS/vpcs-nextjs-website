New Mexico has a long and rich military history. The state hosted key facilities of the Manhattan Project, where the world's first atomic bomb was developed, and was the site of the Trinity nuclear test in 1945. That legacy continues today in missions ranging from nuclear deterrence to special operations and hypersonic testing.

The military bases in New Mexico number four major installations: three Air Force bases and one sprawling Army test range. Here is what each one does, plus what military families should know before a Permanent Change of Station (PCS) move to the Land of Enchantment.

## List of Military Bases in New Mexico

- Cannon Air Force Base (Clovis)
- Holloman Air Force Base (Alamogordo)
- Kirtland Air Force Base (Albuquerque)
- White Sands Missile Range (near Las Cruces)

## 1. Cannon Air Force Base

**Location:** Clovis, NM. **Mission:** Special operations.

Established in 1942 as Clovis Army Air Base and renamed in 1957, Cannon AFB is home to the 27th Special Operations Wing, which conducts special operations missions worldwide. Key features include the roughly 70,000-acre Melrose Air Force Range, used for air-to-ground combat, small arms, and electronic warfare training.

Cannon sits in the high plains of eastern New Mexico, where housing costs are among the most affordable of any Air Force assignment. See our full [PCS guide to Cannon AFB and Clovis](/blog/pcs-to-cannon-afb-clovis-2026-guide) for neighborhoods, schools, and Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH) math. You can learn more at the official [Cannon Air Force Base site](https://www.cannon.af.mil/).

## 2. Holloman Air Force Base

**Location:** Alamogordo, NM. **Mission:** F-16 and MQ-9 aircrew training.

Established in 1942 as Alamogordo Army Air Field, Holloman AFB is home to the 49th Wing, which trains F-16 fighter pilots along with MQ-9 Reaper pilots and sensor operators for the Air Force and allied nations. In June 2026, the wing [transitioned from Air Education and Training Command to Air Combat Command](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/567007/49th-wing-welcomes-new-commander-and-reassigns-air-combat-command) as part of a move to streamline the fighter training pipeline.

Holloman also hosts the famous Holloman High Speed Test Track, a rocket-sled track measuring about 9.6 miles — the [846th Test Squadron](https://www.holloman.af.mil/About/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/317306/846th-test-squadron/) set the world land speed record for rocket sleds there at 6,453 mph in 2003.

Families PCSing here live mostly in Alamogordo, with White Sands National Park minutes away. Our [Holloman AFB and Alamogordo PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-holloman-afb-alamogordo-2026-guide) covers housing and BAH in detail.

## 3. Kirtland Air Force Base

**Location:** Albuquerque, NM. **Mission:** Nuclear deterrence, research, and special operations training.

Kirtland AFB, whose airfield history dates to the early 1940s, is one of the largest bases in Air Force Global Strike Command by mission footprint. It is home to the 377th Air Base Wing, the Air Force Nuclear Weapons Center, which manages nuclear weapons sustainment and modernization, and it hosts Sandia National Laboratories, one of the nation's premier national security research labs.

Kirtland is the one New Mexico base inside a major metro. Albuquerque offers big-city amenities, and many families weigh city neighborhoods against East Mountains communities. Start with our [Kirtland AFB and Albuquerque PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-kirtland-afb-albuquerque-2026-guide), and see the official [Kirtland Air Force Base site](https://www.kirtland.af.mil/) for gate and newcomer information.

## 4. White Sands Missile Range (WSMR)

**Location:** Southern New Mexico, about 30 miles northeast of Las Cruces. **Mission:** Missile and weapons testing.

Established in 1945 as White Sands Proving Ground, [White Sands Missile Range](https://home.army.mil/wsmr/) is the largest military installation in the contiguous United States, covering roughly 3,200 square miles. The Army post supports missile testing and development, training on advanced weapons systems, and aerospace research for the Department of Defense and NASA. The Trinity Site, where the first atomic bomb was tested, sits on the range and opens to visitors on limited days each year.

Most WSMR families live in Las Cruces, a college town with a low cost of living and quick access to El Paso and Fort Bliss just across the Texas line.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many military bases are in New Mexico?

Four major installations: Cannon Air Force Base, Holloman Air Force Base, Kirtland Air Force Base, and White Sands Missile Range. Fort Bliss, headquartered in El Paso, Texas, also extends its training areas into southern New Mexico.

### What is the largest military base in New Mexico?

By land area, White Sands Missile Range — about 3,200 square miles, making it the largest military installation in the contiguous United States.

### Does New Mexico have an Army base?

White Sands Missile Range is the state's Army installation, focused on testing rather than large troop formations. Most soldiers assigned in the region fall under Fort Bliss in neighboring El Paso.

### Is New Mexico a good duty station for buying a home?

Often, yes. Home prices in Clovis, Alamogordo, and Las Cruces run well below national levels, BAH typically stretches further than in coastal markets, and veteran property tax exemptions add long-term savings.
