They range from Fort Bragg, the Army's largest installation, to Camp Lejeune on the coast and a Coast Guard aviation hub in Elizabeth City. If you have PCS (Permanent Change of Station) orders to North Carolina, this guide covers what each base does, where it sits, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like nearby.

## Army Bases in North Carolina

For a deeper look at the Army side, including housing pay and neighborhoods, see our full guide to [Army bases in North Carolina](/blog/army-bases-in-north-carolina).

### Fort Bragg

Fort Bragg, next to Fayetteville, is the largest installation in the Army. It was renamed Fort Liberty in 2023, then renamed Fort Bragg again on March 7, 2025, this time honoring Pfc. Roland L. Bragg, a World War II Silver Star recipient, per the [Army's report on the redesignation](https://www.army.mil/article/284168/back_to_bragg_fort_bragg_redesignation_ceremony).

Major units at Fort Bragg include the XVIII Airborne Corps, the 82nd Airborne Division, U.S. Army Special Operations Command and Womack Army Medical Center, per the [Fort Bragg units page](https://home.army.mil/bragg/units). In December 2025, the Army also stood up U.S. Army Western Hemisphere Command, a four-star headquarters at Fort Bragg, per the [Army's activation report](https://www.army.mil/article/289410/us_army_activates_western_hemisphere_command_in_historic_transition_ceremony). Camp Mackall, a training area tied to Fort Bragg, hosts special operations training.

Moving here? Read our [Fort Bragg 2026 PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-bragg-2026-guide) and our [Fort Bragg BAH and buy vs. rent breakdown](/blog/fort-bragg-bah-2026-buy-vs-rent).

### Pope Army Airfield

Pope Army Airfield is part of Fort Bragg. It is home to the Air Force's 43rd Air Mobility Operations Group, which helps the XVIII Airborne Corps and 82nd Airborne Division deploy fast, per the [Pope Army Airfield About page](https://www.pope.af.mil/About-Us/About-Pope/).

### Military Ocean Terminal Sunny Point

Military Ocean Terminal Sunny Point sits on the west bank of the Cape Fear River. The Army calls it the nation's largest ocean terminal for shipping military equipment and the East Coast's strategic ammunition port, per a [2025 Army report on Sunny Point](https://www.army.mil/article/288475/first_imcom_tdaap_black_start_exercise_shines_light_on_motsu).

## Marine Corps Bases in North Carolina

North Carolina has three main Marine Corps installations. For details on each, plus 2026 BAH, see our full guide to [Marine Corps bases in North Carolina](/blog/marine-corps-bases-in-north-carolina).

### Marine Corps Base Camp Lejeune

Camp Lejeune, in Jacksonville, is home to II Marine Expeditionary Force (II MEF), the 2nd Marine Division and the 2nd Marine Logistics Group, per the [II MEF About page](https://www.iimef.marines.mil/About/What-is-II-MEF/). Marine Forces Special Operations Command (MARSOC) is based here, too. For neighborhoods, read our [Camp Lejeune and Onslow County guide](/blog/pcsing-to-camp-lejeune-2026-onslow-county-guide).

### MCAS New River

Marine Corps Air Station New River sits next to Camp Lejeune. It is home to MV-22 Osprey and CH-53 helicopter squadrons, per the [2nd Marine Aircraft Wing's VMM-263 page](https://www.2ndmaw.marines.mil/Units/MAG-26/VMM-263/).

### MCAS Cherry Point

MCAS Cherry Point, near Havelock, is headquarters for the 2nd Marine Aircraft Wing, per the [2nd MAW About page](https://www.2ndmaw.marines.mil/Home/About/). It is moving from the AV-8B Harrier to the F-35B. It is also home to Fleet Readiness Center East, a large aircraft repair depot. See our [MCAS Cherry Point PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-mcas-cherry-point-2026-guide).

## Air Force Bases in North Carolina

### Seymour Johnson Air Force Base

Seymour Johnson AFB is in Goldsboro. Its 4th Fighter Wing flies the F-15E Strike Eagle and was the first operational F-15E wing, per the [4th Fighter Wing fact sheet](https://www.seymourjohnson.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/article/306531/). The base also hosts the Air Force Reserve's 916th Air Refueling Wing, the Reserve's first KC-46A Pegasus wing, per the [916th Air Refueling Wing page](https://www.afrc.af.mil/Units/916arw/). Our [Seymour Johnson AFB PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-seymour-johnson-afb-goldsboro-2026-guide) covers Goldsboro housing.

## Coast Guard Bases in North Carolina

### Coast Guard Air Station Elizabeth City and Aviation Logistics Center

Coast Guard Base Elizabeth City is the Coast Guard's largest aviation hub on the East Coast, per a [July 2026 Coast Guard news release](https://www.news.uscg.mil/Press-Releases/Article/4535030/coast-guard-breaks-ground-on-runway-1-19-recapitalization-at-base-elizabeth-city/). It includes Air Station Elizabeth City, which flies MH-60 Jayhawk helicopters and HC-130J airplanes for search and rescue. It also includes the Aviation Logistics Center, the Coast Guard's only aviation industrial complex. The center handles depot-level repair, engineering and supply for all Coast Guard aircraft.

### Coast Guard Sector North Carolina

Coast Guard Sector North Carolina oversees search and rescue, port safety and law enforcement along the state's coast, per the [Coast Guard's Sector North Carolina page](https://www.atlanticarea.uscg.mil/Our-Organization/East-District/District-Units/Sector-North-Carolina/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many military bases are in North Carolina?

North Carolina has 10 major military installations across the Army, Marine Corps, Air Force and Coast Guard. The biggest are Fort Bragg and Camp Lejeune.

### What Army bases are in North Carolina?

Fort Bragg, next to Fayetteville, is the state's main Army post and home of the 82nd Airborne Division. Pope Army Airfield is part of Fort Bragg. The Army also runs Military Ocean Terminal Sunny Point on the Cape Fear River.

### What Marine Corps bases are in North Carolina?

Camp Lejeune, MCAS New River and MCAS Cherry Point. Lejeune and New River are in Jacksonville, and Cherry Point is near Havelock.

### Is Fort Liberty still called Fort Liberty?

No. The post was renamed Fort Bragg on March 7, 2025. It now honors Pfc. Roland L. Bragg, a World War II hero.

### What is the largest military base in North Carolina?

Fort Bragg. The Army calls it the largest installation in the Army.
