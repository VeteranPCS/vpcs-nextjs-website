If you are researching Air Force bases in Georgia, you have more options than many people expect. The state has two active-duty bases, Robins Air Force Base (AFB) in Warner Robins and Moody AFB in Valdosta. It also has two busy part-time bases: Dobbins Air Reserve Base (ARB) near Atlanta and the Air National Guard base in Savannah. This guide covers what each one does, where it sits, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like nearby.

## Robins AFB in Warner Robins

Robins AFB sits in Houston County in Middle Georgia, south of Macon. Its host unit is the 78th Air Base Wing. The base has more than 22,000 military, civilian and contract workers and more than 50 mission partners. Its yearly economic impact is about $4.26 billion, per the [Robins AFB fact sheet](https://www.robins.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/2843093/robins-air-force-base/), updated in 2025. The base calls itself "Georgia's largest single-site industrial complex."

The biggest tenant is the Warner Robins Air Logistics Complex. About 6,000 workers there repair and update the F-15, C-5, C-130, C-17 and special operations aircraft, per the [Air Logistics Complex page](https://www.robins.af.mil/Units/WR-Air-Logistics-Complex/). That means many civilian jobs, which can help military spouses find work.

Robins is also home to Headquarters Air Force Reserve Command, the 461st Air Control Wing and the Georgia Air National Guard's 116th Air Control Wing. The two air control wings flew the E-8C JSTARS radar jet for more than 20 years. That fleet has retired, and the base has been adding new battle management and communications missions, per an [Air Force report on new Robins missions](https://www.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/3236768/robins-afb-takes-next-step-in-establishing-new-missions/). Some of those plans are still shifting in the budget, so ask your gaining unit what to expect.

## Moody AFB in Valdosta

Moody AFB sits just northeast of Valdosta in South Georgia. Its host unit is the 23d Wing, which has 21 squadrons, per the [23d Wing page](https://www.moody.af.mil/About-Us/Units/23rd-Wing/). The wing has two main flying groups:

- The 23d Fighter Group flies the A-10C Thunderbolt II, a tough jet built for close air support of troops on the ground.
- The 347th Rescue Group flies the HH-60W Jolly Green II helicopter and the HC-130J Combat King II, and it also has a Guardian Angel pararescue squadron.

**What may change.** The Air Force plans to replace Moody's A-10s with two F-35A squadrons, timed with A-10 retirement. A draft environmental impact statement was released in 2026, and the plan would add about 165 people above today's level, per [Moody AFB's notice on the draft environmental impact statement](https://www.moody.af.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/4585345/draft-environmental-impact-statement-notice-of-availability/). No final decision had been announced as of November 2026, so treat this as a proposal.

For housing and schools around Valdosta, read our [Moody AFB 2026 PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-moody-afb-valdosta-2026-guide).

## Dobbins Air Reserve Base in Marietta

Dobbins ARB is about 16 miles northwest of downtown Atlanta. Its host unit, the 94th Airlift Wing, flies eight C-130H3 Hercules cargo planes, per the [Dobbins ARB fact sheets page](https://www.dobbins.afrc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets). The base also hosts the headquarters of 22nd Air Force and the Georgia National Guard, and Lockheed Martin uses the base airfield, per the [Dobbins ARB fact sheet](https://www.dobbins.afrc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/170612/dobbins-air-reserve-base/) (last updated in 2020). Like most Reserve bases, much of its workforce serves part time.

## Savannah Air National Guard Base

The Georgia Air National Guard's 165th Airlift Wing flies out of Savannah. It is moving from older C-130H planes to the C-130J-30 Super Hercules, and its first C-130J-30 arrived on January 22, 2024, per a [National Guard Bureau report](https://www.nationalguard.mil/News/Article-View/Article/3652193/georgia-air-national-guard-receives-its-1st-c-130j-30-aircraft).

## What BAH Covers Near Georgia's Air Force Bases

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off base. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Robins AFB | GA076 | $1,800 / $1,491 | $1,992 / $1,695 | $2,199 / $1,842 |
| Moody AFB | GA081 | $1,524 / $1,416 | $1,950 / $1,512 | $2,004 / $1,773 |
| Dobbins ARB (Atlanta) | GA071 | $2,388 / $1,995 | $2,730 / $2,262 | $2,745 / $2,589 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 at Robins AFB, Moody AFB and Dobbins ARB](/images/blog/air-force-bases-in-georgia/georgia-air-force-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near Georgia's Air Force installations. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- Dobbins uses the Atlanta rate, so it pays the most. An E-5 with dependents gets $588 more a month there than at Robins.
- Robins pays more than Moody at every grade shown. An E-5 with dependents gets $276 more a month at Robins.
- At Moody, an E-7 with dependents gets $438 more a month than an E-7 without dependents.

Higher BAH near Atlanta also comes with higher home prices, so compare the whole picture. To turn your rate into a price range, read [how much house you can afford on BAH](/blog/how-much-house-can-you-afford-on-bah).

## Buying a Home Near Georgia's Air Force Bases

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the home's appraised value, and you do not pay private mortgage insurance. To compare it with other loan types, see [VA loan vs. conventional loan](/blog/va-loan-vs-conventional-loan).

**Know the Georgia tax rules.** Military retirees under age 62 can exclude up to $17,500 of military retirement pay from Georgia income tax. They can exclude up to $17,500 more if they also earn more than $17,500 in Georgia, per the [Georgia Department of Revenue's retirement income exclusion page](https://dor.georgia.gov/retirement-income-exclusion). Veterans with a qualifying service-connected disability may get a large homestead exemption that rises each year. It was $121,812 of assessed value for 2025, per the [Georgia Department of Veterans Service](https://veterans.georgia.gov/disabled-veteran-homestead-tax-exemption). You apply at your county tax office. See our guide to [Georgia veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/georgia-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

**Check noise zones.** Near Moody and Robins, ask your agent to check aircraft noise zones before you make an offer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Air Force bases are in Georgia?

Georgia has two active-duty Air Force bases, Robins AFB and Moody AFB. It also has Dobbins Air Reserve Base near Atlanta and an Air National Guard base in Savannah.

### What is the largest Air Force base in Georgia?

Robins AFB. It has more than 22,000 workers and calls itself Georgia's largest single-site industrial complex.

### What planes fly at Moody AFB?

Moody flies the A-10C Thunderbolt II, the HH-60W Jolly Green II and the HC-130J Combat King II. The Air Force has proposed replacing the A-10s with F-35As in the future.

### Which Georgia Air Force base has the highest BAH?

Dobbins ARB, because it uses the Atlanta rate. In 2026 an E-5 with dependents gets $2,388 a month there.
