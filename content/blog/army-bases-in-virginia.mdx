The list of army bases in Virginia is short enough to learn in one sitting. The hard part is that three of these posts were renamed in 2023 and renamed again in 2025, so your orders and the sign at the gate may not match.

This guide covers every Army installation in the state, what each does, and what housing costs nearby. PCS stands for Permanent Change of Station, the military's term for an assigned move.

## Every Army Installation in Virginia, at a Glance

Eight places in Virginia are Army installations or Army land. Only four are common duty stations for families.

| Installation | Where it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fort Belvoir | Fairfax County, south of Washington, D.C. | Headquarters campus for defense agencies and Army commands |
| Fort Eustis | Newport News | Army half of Joint Base Langley-Eustis; home of Army Training and Doctrine Command |
| Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall | Arlington, plus Fort McNair in Washington, D.C. | Ceremonial and support post for the National Capital Region |
| Fort Lee | Prince George County, next to Petersburg | Sustainment training: quartermaster, ordnance, transportation |
| Fort Pickett | Blackstone, Nottoway County | Virginia National Guard maneuver training center |
| Fort A.P. Hill | Caroline County, near Bowling Green | Large maneuver and live-fire training area |
| Fort Story | Virginia Beach | Army land, now part of Navy-led Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek-Fort Story |
| Radford Army Ammunition Plant | Radford, southwest Virginia | Makes propellants and energetics; not a family duty station |

## What Each Post Does

### Fort Belvoir

Fort Belvoir covers an 8,656-acre tract along the Potomac in Fairfax County, about 20 miles south of Washington, D.C. It is a headquarters campus, not a troop post: more than 145 mission partners serving more than 216,000 service members, civilians, retirees and families, per [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/in-depth-overview/fort-belvoir).

Tenants include the Defense Logistics Agency, the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency, Army Cyber Command and the National Museum of the United States Army. See our [Fort Belvoir PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-belvoir-2026-guide).

### Fort Eustis

Fort Eustis in Newport News is the Army half of Joint Base Langley-Eustis, written JBLE, which pairs it with Air Force-run Langley in Hampton. It is the headquarters of U.S. Army Training and Doctrine Command, or TRADOC, which runs Army training and writes Army doctrine. It also hosts the 7th Transportation Brigade (Expeditionary), the 128th Aviation Brigade and aviation logistics training, per the joint base's [Army units page](https://www.jble.af.mil/Units/Army/). One detail catches new arrivals: garrison services on the Eustis side are run by the Air Force's 733d Mission Support Group. See our [Joint Base Langley-Eustis PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-joint-base-langley-eustis-2026-guide).

### Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall

JBM-HH stands for Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall. It is Army-commanded and made of three properties: Fort Myer in Arlington, the Marine Corps' Henderson Hall next door, and Fort McNair in southwest Washington, D.C. Fort Myer and Henderson Hall run along the west edge of Arlington National Cemetery.

It is home to the 3d U.S. Infantry Regiment, The Old Guard, which provides the honor guard for funerals at Arlington. The U.S. Army Band and National Defense University are here too, per the garrison's [about page](https://home.army.mil/jbmhh/about).

### Fort Lee

Fort Lee sits beside the Tri-Cities of Petersburg, Colonial Heights and Hopewell. It is the Army's Sustainment Center of Excellence: supply, food service, maintenance, ammunition and transportation. Team Lee includes the Combined Arms Support Command, or CASCOM, plus Army Sustainment University and the Ordnance, Quartermaster and Transportation schools.

Daily population averages more than 29,000, and up to 70,000 troops pass through its classrooms yearly, making it the [third largest training site in the Army](https://home.army.mil/lee/about). Our [Fort Lee PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-lee-2026-guide) covers arrival.

### Fort Pickett

Fort Pickett, near Blackstone, is a Virginia National Guard installation: about 41,000 acres of maneuver terrain, 21 ranges, barracks for more than 5,000 and an Army airfield, per the [Virginia National Guard](https://va.ng.mil/Installations/Fort-Pickett/). Active-duty families rarely get orders here.

### Fort A.P. Hill

Fort A.P. Hill covers roughly 76,000 acres in Caroline County, including a 28,000-acre live-fire range complex, per its [garrison history page](https://home.army.mil/aphill/about/history). Units come here to train, not to live.

### Fort Story

Fort Story in Virginia Beach is still Army land, but since October 1, 2009 it has run as half of Navy-led Joint Expeditionary Base Little Creek-Fort Story, which [Navy Region Mid-Atlantic](https://cnrma.cnic.navy.mil/Installations/JEB-Little-Creek-Fort-Story/About/History/) calls one command with two properties. Army units still train on the beach, but the gates and base services are Navy-run. See our [Virginia Beach PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-nas-oceana-jeb-little-creek-virginia-beach-2026-guide).

### Radford Army Ammunition Plant

Radford Army Ammunition Plant, in southwest Virginia, makes propellants, energetics and munitions for the Army's [Joint Munitions Command](https://www.jmc.army.mil/Radford/RadfordDefault.aspx). It is an industrial site, not a duty station.

### One thing that is not an Army post

The Pentagon in Arlington is Department of Defense headquarters, documented by the [DoD Historical Office](https://history.defense.gov/DOD-DOW-History/Pentagon/History/). Soldiers work there, but it is not an Army installation and has no garrison.

## The Base Names That Changed Twice

On June 10, 2025, the Army announced it would [change the names of seven installations](https://www.army.mil/article/286223/army_to_change_names_of_seven_installations), three of them in Virginia. Fort Gregg-Adams became Fort Lee again, Fort Barfoot became Fort Pickett again, and Fort Walker became Fort A.P. Hill again.

The spellings went back, but the namesakes did not. Fort Lee now honors Pvt. Fitz Lee, a Buffalo Soldier awarded the Medal of Honor in the Spanish-American War. Fort Pickett honors 1st Lt. Vernon W. Pickett, a Distinguished Service Cross recipient from World War II. Fort A.P. Hill honors three Civil War Medal of Honor recipients: Lt. Col. Edward Hill, 1st Sgt. Robert A. Pinn and Pvt. Bruce Anderson.

So the correct names today are Fort Lee, Fort Pickett and Fort A.P. Hill. You will still see the 2023 names on older orders and map apps. Same gates, same ZIP codes.

## What Housing Costs Around Each Post

BAH is Basic Allowance for Housing, the monthly amount the military pays you toward housing off post. It is set by pay grade, dependent status and your duty station's rate area, not by the rent you actually pay.

Here are 2026 monthly rates for three common pay grades, with dependents first and without second, from the official [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/) run by the Defense Travel Management Office.

| Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall | Washington DC Metro Area (DC053) | $3,132 / $2,832 | $3,855 / $3,099 | $4,020 / $3,531 |
| Fort Lee | Richmond, VA (VA301) | $2,358 / $1,866 | $2,676 / $2,163 | $2,760 / $2,526 |
| Fort A.P. Hill | Camp A. P. Hill, VA (VA368) | $2,313 / $2,196 | $2,763 / $2,325 | $2,844 / $2,568 |
| Fort Pickett | County Cost Group 710 (ZZ710) | $1,515 / $1,227 | $1,890 / $1,419 | $2,016 / $1,704 |

Two things are worth naming. First, the DTMO lookup still labels the Caroline County rate area "Camp A. P. Hill, VA (VA368)." The post is Fort A.P. Hill; the label has not caught up. Look for the old wording so you do not think your rate is missing.

Second, the spread. An E-5 with dependents draws $3,132 near Washington and $1,515 near Fort Pickett. That is more than $1,600 a month of difference inside one state, the widest in-state gap most Virginia families will see.

Fort Eustis is not in the table because we could not confirm its 2026 figure; look it up by ZIP code in the DTMO tool. Rates reset every January, so check your own pay grade rather than a friend's.

## Virginia Is Four Housing Markets, Not One

**Northern Virginia.** Fairfax, Prince William and Arlington are the priciest part of the state, which is why the allowance is highest here. Fort Belvoir families often look at Woodbridge, Lorton and Springfield; JBM-HH families usually rent.

**Richmond and the Tri-Cities.** Fort Lee families get the most comfortable math in the state. Colonial Heights, Chester, Hopewell and Prince George offer real houses at prices a mid-career enlisted family can carry.

**Hampton Roads.** Fort Eustis shares a dense market with Navy and Air Force families across Newport News, Yorktown and Williamsburg. Inventory turns fast in summer, and flood zones and insurance costs matter here in a way they do not inland.

**Rural Southside.** Around Fort Pickett and Fort A.P. Hill, prices are low, inventory is thin and commutes run in country miles.

Wherever you land, check whether you qualify for a property tax break. Virginia exempts veterans with a permanent and total service-connected disability from real estate tax on a primary home; see our guide to [Virginia veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/virginia-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Army bases are in Virginia?

Eight: Fort Belvoir, Fort Eustis, Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall, Fort Lee, Fort Pickett, Fort A.P. Hill, Fort Story and Radford Army Ammunition Plant. Only the first four are common family duty stations.

### What is the biggest Army base in Virginia?

It depends how you measure. By land, Fort A.P. Hill is largest at roughly 76,000 acres, but almost nobody lives there. By people, Fort Belvoir wins, serving more than 216,000. By training volume, Fort Lee leads.

### Is Fort Gregg-Adams still called Fort Gregg-Adams?

No. The Army announced the change back to Fort Lee on June 10, 2025, and held the ceremony that July. The name now honors Pvt. Fitz Lee, a Medal of Honor recipient. Older paperwork may still say Fort Gregg-Adams.

### Is Fort Eustis an Army base or an Air Force base?

Both. Fort Eustis is Army land and home to Army Training and Doctrine Command, but it is half of Joint Base Langley-Eustis, and garrison services there are Air Force-run.

### What is BAH at Fort Belvoir in 2026?

Fort Belvoir sits in the Washington DC Metro Area rate area (DC053). For 2026 the with-dependents rate is $3,132 for an E-5, $3,855 for an E-7 and $4,020 for an O-3. Joint Base Myer-Henderson Hall matches. Confirm your pay grade in the DTMO lookup; rates reset every January.
