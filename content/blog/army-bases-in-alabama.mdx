## Redstone Arsenal

Redstone Arsenal sits on the southwest side of Huntsville. Per the [Military OneSource Redstone Arsenal overview](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/in-depth-overview/redstone-arsenal), it covers about 38,000 acres. Its workforce is about 45,500 people. Only about 900 are active duty or reserve. The rest are about 27,300 civilians and just over 17,000 contractors.

That mix shapes the whole area. Redstone is less a troop post and more a giant hub for defense, space and research jobs. Per the [Redstone units and tenants page](https://home.army.mil/redstone/units-tenants), tenants include:

- Army Materiel Command (AMC) headquarters.
- Aviation and Missile Command (AMCOM).
- Space and Missile Defense Command (SMDC).
- The Missile Defense Agency.
- Army Contracting Command.
- NASA's Marshall Space Flight Center.
- FBI and ATF explosives training and analysis centers.

### U.S. Space Command Is Moving In

The biggest news at Redstone is U.S. Space Command. The headquarters is moving from Colorado Springs to Huntsville. Per a [U.S. Space Command news release](https://www.spacecom.mil/Newsroom/News/Article-Display/Article/4473792/us-space-command-takes-operational-control-of-facility-at-redstone-arsenal/), the move was announced on September 2, 2025. The first staff started moving in April 2026, and the command took control of its first Redstone building on April 29, 2026. The goal is for at least half the command to work from Redstone by the end of 2028.

The move is happening in stages. If it affects your orders, confirm your report date and duty location before you sign a lease or buy a home.

## Fort Rucker

If you are an Army aviator, you have probably been to Fort Rucker. It is home to the U.S. Army Aviation Center of Excellence. Per an [army.mil article on the redesignation](https://www.army.mil/article/287149/home_of_army_aviation_redesignated_fort_rucker), the post was called Fort Novosel from 2023 to 2025. The Army changed the name back to Fort Rucker in July 2025. It now honors Capt. Edward W. Rucker Jr., a World War I pilot who earned the Distinguished Service Cross.

Per the [Military OneSource Fort Rucker overview](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/in-depth-overview/fort-rucker), the post covers about 64,500 acres in Dale County. It has gates facing three towns:

- Enterprise, to the west.
- Daleville, to the south.
- Ozark, to the east.

Fort Rucker is about 20 miles northwest of Dothan. About 1,250 new aviators a year go through initial helicopter training here, per an [army.mil article on Flight School Next](https://www.army.mil/article/294614/army_announces_flight_school_next_selection_to_modernize_aviator_training). Flight school students and instructors often PCS on short timelines, so many families rent first.

For housing and school details, see our [PCS guide to Fort Rucker](/blog/pcs-to-fort-rucker-2026-guide).

## Anniston Army Depot

Anniston Army Depot is in Calhoun County, about 60 miles east of Birmingham along I-20. It calls itself "The Tank Rebuild Center of the World." Per the [Military OneSource Anniston Army Depot overview](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/in-depth-overview/anniston-army-depot), it covers more than 25 square miles and has 3,232 depot employees.

Most of its work is on the Abrams tank, per a [2026 GAO (Government Accountability Office) report summary](https://www.gao.gov/blog/where-does-army-go-repair-tank). The depot also repairs other tracked and wheeled combat vehicles, artillery, small arms and even locomotives.

Nearby Fort McClellan closed in 1999 under BRAC (Base Realignment and Closure). Most of its land now belongs to the local community.

## What BAH Covers Near Alabama Army Bases

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off base. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Huntsville (Redstone Arsenal) | AL003 | $1,797 / $1,443 | $2,133 / $1,671 | $2,226 / $1,974 |
| Fort Rucker | AL002 | $1,572 / $1,251 | $1,929 / $1,446 | $2,262 / $1,767 |
| Anniston | AL001 | $1,185 / $1,119 | $1,560 / $1,191 | $1,686 / $1,377 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 in the Huntsville, Fort Rucker and Anniston rate areas](/images/blog/army-bases-in-alabama/alabama-army-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near Alabama's Army bases. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- Huntsville pays the most at E-5 and E-7. At E-5 with dependents, it pays $225 more a month than Fort Rucker.
- At O-3 with dependents, the Fort Rucker rate is $36 higher than the Huntsville rate.
- Anniston has the lowest rates of the three, but homes there also tend to cost less.

## Buying a Home Near Alabama Army Bases

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the appraised value. There is no private mortgage insurance. Buying in Huntsville or Madison? Read our [VA loan guide for Madison County](/blog/va-loan-huntsville-madison-county-2026).

**Know the tax picture.** Per the [Alabama Department of Revenue](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/individual-corporate/income-exempt-from-alabama-income-taxation/), military retirement pay is exempt from Alabama income tax. Alabama property taxes are low, too. The Tax Foundation's [2024 property tax data](https://taxfoundation.org/data/all/state/how-high-are-property-taxes-your-state/) put Alabama's effective rate on owner-occupied homes at 0.43%, the second-lowest in the country.

**Ask about disabled veteran exemptions.** Alabama's [homestead exemption](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/property-tax/homestead-exemptions/) may cover property taxes on the main home of someone who is permanently and totally disabled. Under [HB77](https://alison.legislature.state.al.us/bill-search?search=HB77&tab=1), effective October 1, 2026, veterans with a VA rating of 100% permanent and total service-connected disability can ask the local tax office for a tentative certificate before closing. The certificate can keep eligible property taxes out of the mortgage debt-to-income calculation. See our [Alabama veteran property tax guide](/blog/alabama-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

If Air Force orders to Montgomery are also possible, read our [PCS guide to Maxwell AFB](/blog/pcs-to-maxwell-afb-montgomery-2026-guide). If you are coming from Colorado with Space Command, our guide to [Army bases in Colorado](/blog/army-bases-in-colorado) covers the other end of the move.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Where should I start looking for housing near Fort Rucker?

Compare Enterprise, Daleville and Ozark. Ask your sponsor which gate you will use regularly, then check the commute and look for a lease that fits your assignment.

### Is it Fort Rucker or Fort Novosel?

It is Fort Rucker again. The post was named Fort Novosel from 2023 to 2025, and the Army restored the Fort Rucker name in July 2025.

### Is U.S. Space Command moving to Huntsville?

Yes. The move to Redstone Arsenal was announced in September 2025. The first staff started moving in April 2026, and the goal is for at least half the command to work there by the end of 2028.

### What is the BAH for an E-5 at Redstone Arsenal?

In 2026, an E-5 with dependents gets $1,797 a month in the Huntsville rate area (AL003). Without dependents, the rate is $1,443.

### Does Alabama tax military retirement pay?

No. The Alabama Department of Revenue lists military retirement pay as exempt from state income tax.
