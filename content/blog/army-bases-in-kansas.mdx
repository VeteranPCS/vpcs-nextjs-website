There are two Army bases in Kansas: Fort Riley in the Flint Hills and Fort Leavenworth on the Missouri River. They sit at opposite ends of the drive from Kansas City into the Flint Hills, and they feel like different worlds. One is home to a combat division. The other is where the Army sends mid-career officers to school.

This guide covers what each post does, who gets orders there, and what housing costs nearby. PCS stands for Permanent Change of Station, the military's term for an assigned move.

## The Army Bases in Kansas, at a Glance

| Installation | Where it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fort Riley | Between Junction City and Manhattan | Home of the 1st Infantry Division, the Big Red One |
| Fort Leavenworth | Leavenworth, northwest of Kansas City | Combined Arms Center and the Command and General Staff College |

## Fort Riley: Home of the Big Red One

### Where it is

Fort Riley sits in the Central Flint Hills of northeast Kansas, about an hour west of Topeka and two and a half hours from Kansas City. The nearest towns are Junction City, Ogden and Manhattan, per the [Fort Riley garrison](https://home.army.mil/riley/about).

### What it does

Fort Riley is home to the 1st Infantry Division, known as the Big Red One. It is a combat division, so expect deployments, field time and a young, busy community. The post also supports National Guard and Reserve Soldiers from Kansas, Oklahoma, Missouri, Nebraska and Iowa.

### How big it is

The post covers 101,733 acres, and 91,597 of those acres are training land. It serves more than 67,000 people: about 15,400 active-duty members, 19,600 family members, 6,100 civilian employees and 26,000 retirees and veterans in the region.

## Fort Leavenworth: The Army's Schoolhouse

### Where it is

Fort Leavenworth sits on the bluffs above the Missouri River, next to the city of Leavenworth and northwest of Kansas City. Missouri is right across the river.

### What it does

Fort Leavenworth is headquarters of the Army's Combined Arms Center, which the garrison calls the Intellectual Center of the Army. It is home to the Command and General Staff College, part of the [Army University](https://www.armyupress.army.mil/Portals/7/home/the-army-university-overview-booklet.pdf), where mid-career officers attend professional military education.

Because so many moves are tied to class start dates, the rental market can get tight in summer. If you report in June or July, start your search early.

### Its history

Founded in 1827, Fort Leavenworth is the oldest continuously operating military installation west of the Mississippi River and the oldest permanent settlement in Kansas, per the [Fort Leavenworth garrison](https://home.army.mil/leavenworth/about). Its motto is "The Best Hometown in the Army." Our [Fort Leavenworth PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-leavenworth-complete-guide-for-military-families) covers the move itself.

## What BAH Covers at Fort Riley and Fort Leavenworth

BAH is Basic Allowance for Housing, the monthly amount the military pays you toward housing when you live off post. It is set by pay grade, dependent status and your duty station's rate area, not by the rent you actually pay.

Fort Riley has its own rate area (KS100), and so does Fort Leavenworth (KS102). Here are 2026 monthly rates, with dependents first and without second, from the official [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/).

| Pay grade | Fort Riley (KS100) | Fort Leavenworth (KS102) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| E-4 | $1,272 / $1,050 | $1,740 / $1,410 |
| E-5 | $1,314 / $1,158 | $1,815 / $1,569 |
| E-7 | $1,845 / $1,383 | $2,229 / $1,737 |
| O-3 | $1,953 / $1,611 | $2,394 / $2,013 |
| O-4 | $2,169 / $1,842 | $2,508 / $2,226 |

![Bar chart comparing 2026 Basic Allowance for Housing with dependents at Fort Riley and Fort Leavenworth for E-4, E-5, E-7, O-3 and O-4 pay grades](/images/blog/army-bases-in-kansas/kansas-army-bah-2026.png)

*2026 Basic Allowance for Housing, with dependents, for the Fort Riley and Fort Leavenworth rate areas. Source: DoD DTMO BAH rate lookup.*

Two things to notice. First, Leavenworth pays more at every grade. An E-5 with dependents draws $501 a month more at Fort Leavenworth than at Fort Riley, because the post sits on the edge of the Kansas City housing market.

Second, at Fort Riley the E-4 and E-5 rates are only $42 apart. A promotion from E-4 to E-5 there adds little to your housing allowance, so plan your budget around the rate you have today.

Rates reset every January. Look up your own pay grade and ZIP code, and see our explainer on [2026 BAH rates](/blog/2026-bah-rates-what-changed-and-how-to-check-yours) for how the lookup works.

## Where Families Tend to Live

**Around Fort Riley.** Junction City sits right outside the main gate and has the shortest commute. Manhattan, home of Kansas State University, offers more restaurants and a college-town feel. Ogden, Grandview Plaza and Wamego are smaller, quieter options.

**Around Fort Leavenworth.** Leavenworth and Lansing are the closest Kansas towns. Some families live across the river in Platte County, Missouri, or commute from the Kansas City suburbs. If you live in Missouri, check how each state treats military income before you decide.

If you plan to buy, a VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs that usually needs no down payment. ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Army bases are in Kansas?

Two: Fort Riley and Fort Leavenworth. McConnell Air Force Base in Wichita is an Air Force base, not Army.

### What is the largest Army base in Kansas?

Fort Riley, at 101,733 acres. It is home to the 1st Infantry Division and serves more than 67,000 people.

### What is BAH at Fort Riley in 2026?

Fort Riley is in rate area KS100. For 2026 the with-dependents rate is $1,314 for an E-5, $1,845 for an E-7 and $1,953 for an O-3.

### How far apart are Fort Riley and Fort Leavenworth?

Plan on a long drive. Fort Riley is about two and a half hours from Kansas City, per the garrison, and Fort Leavenworth sits just northwest of Kansas City.
