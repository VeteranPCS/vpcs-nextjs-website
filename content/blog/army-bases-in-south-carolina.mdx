The Army bases in South Carolina are few, but they matter a lot. Fort Jackson in Columbia trains about half of every new class of Soldiers. Third Army, also called U.S. Army Central, runs its headquarters from Shaw Air Force Base in Sumter. And Army transportation units work the port at Joint Base Charleston. This guide covers what each site does, which town it is near, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like nearby.

## Fort Jackson

Fort Jackson is the heart of the Army in South Carolina. It started in 1917 as Camp Jackson, during World War I, per the [Fort Jackson history page](https://home.army.mil/jackson/about/history). It was named for President Andrew Jackson and became Fort Jackson in 1940. The post covers about 51,316 acres, with more than 1,000 buildings and 100 ranges and field training sites.

Its official name is the U.S. Army Training Center and Fort Jackson. The [Fort Jackson home page](https://home.army.mil/jackson/index.php) calls it the Army's main production center for Basic Combat Training (BCT). Here is what that looks like each year:

- It trains roughly half of all new Soldiers.
- It trains more than 60% of women entering the Army.
- More than 45,000 Soldiers go through basic training here.
- About 12,000 more Soldiers go through advanced training here.

Fort Jackson is also a school campus for leaders. Tenants include the U.S. Army Drill Sergeant Academy, the Soldier Support Institute, the U.S. Army Institute for Religious Leadership and the Leader Training Brigade. The Army moved all drill sergeant training here in 2007, per the history page.

That means many Soldiers PCS to Fort Jackson to serve as drill sergeants, instructors or staff. Our [PCS guide to Fort Jackson and Columbia](/blog/pcs-to-fort-jackson-columbia-2026-guide) covers neighborhoods, schools and commutes.

### Visiting for Basic Training Graduation

If your Soldier is in basic training at Fort Jackson, you may be heading to Columbia for graduation. The Fort Jackson home page lists a general information line, (803) 751-5951, which also helps families locate a trainee. Check the post's [visitor information page](https://home.army.mil/jackson/my-fort/visitor-information) before you travel, since access rules can change.

## U.S. Army Central at Shaw Air Force Base

Not every Army job in South Carolina is at Fort Jackson. U.S. Army Central (USARCENT), also called Third Army, has its headquarters at Patton Hall on Shaw Air Force Base, near Sumter, per the [USARCENT newcomers page](https://www.usarcent.army.mil/Newcomers/). Shaw also hosts Ninth Air Force and U.S. Air Forces Central, the air arm of U.S. Central Command.

The newcomers page says Columbia is about 45 miles from Shaw, under an hour's drive. Soldiers ask for a sponsor using DA Form 5434. Civilian employees in-process at Fort Jackson's Civilian Personnel Advisory Center in Columbia.

If you are headed to Patton Hall, our [PCS guide to Shaw AFB and Sumter](/blog/pcs-to-shaw-afb-sumter-2026-guide) covers housing near the base. For the Air Force side, see [Air Force bases in South Carolina](/blog/air-force-bases-in-south-carolina).

## Army Units at Joint Base Charleston

Joint Base Charleston is run by the Air Force, but the Army has a role there, too. The [Joint Base Charleston snapshot](https://www.jbcharleston.jb.mil/About-Us/JB-CHS-Snapshot/) lists an Army Transportation Detachment among its mission partners.

The 841st Transportation Battalion moves Army gear "from the port to the fort" and back. Its reservists check equipment coming off cargo ships, stage it and track the paperwork, per a [Joint Base Charleston feature on the 841st](https://www.jbcharleston.jb.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/2388784/from-the-port-to-the-fort-841st-transportation-battalion/). That story is from 2020, so confirm current unit details with your gaining command.

## What BAH Covers Near South Carolina Army Sites

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off post. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code, and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fort Jackson and Columbia | SC260 | $1,878 / $1,647 | $2,160 / $1,812 | $2,232 / $2,028 |
| Shaw AFB and Sumter | SC263 | $1,503 / $1,290 | $1,986 / $1,491 | $2,052 / $1,785 |
| Charleston | SC259 | $2,385 / $1,941 | $2,652 / $2,223 | $2,721 / $2,529 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 in the Columbia, Sumter and Charleston rate areas](/images/blog/army-bases-in-south-carolina/south-carolina-army-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near South Carolina's Army sites. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- At Fort Jackson, an E-7 with dependents gets $2,160 a month. That is $348 more than an E-7 without dependents.
- A Soldier assigned to Army Central at Shaw falls in the Sumter rate area, which pays $375 less than Columbia for an E-5 with dependents.
- Charleston pays the most of the three areas at every grade shown.

BAH is meant to cover typical rent and utilities, not your dream home. ## Buying a Home Near South Carolina Army Sites

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the home's appraised value. You also do not need private mortgage insurance. Our [complete guide to buying your first home with a VA loan](/blog/complete-guide-to-buying-your-first-home-with-a-va-loan) walks through each step.

**Think about after the Army.** South Carolina military retirement pay is "exempt from South Carolina Individual Income Tax, no matter the recipient's age," per the [SC Department of Revenue tips for retirees](https://dor.sc.gov/tax-tips/retirees-lower-your-individual-income-tax-bill-these-five-tips). If you might retire here, buying now and keeping the home can make sense. Veterans with a 100% rating may also get property tax help. See our guide to [South Carolina veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/south-carolina-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

**Plan for your next move.** Most military tours end with another PCS. Before you buy, think about whether you would sell or rent the home when you leave. Our guide on whether to [rent or sell your home when you PCS](/blog/rent-or-sell-your-home-when-you-pcs) can help.
