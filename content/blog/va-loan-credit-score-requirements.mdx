Here is the answer most veterans are surprised to hear: the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) does not set a minimum credit score for a VA loan. VA loan credit score requirements come from lenders, not from the VA itself. The [VA's home loan eligibility toolkit](https://benefits.va.gov/homeloans/documents/docs/lgy-eligibility-for-va-home-loan-toolkit.pdf) states that while the VA has no minimum credit score requirement, lenders will use your credit history to judge how you handle debt.

That one fact changes how you should shop. If one lender turns you down, another lender working under the same VA rules may say yes. This guide walks through the score floors lenders actually use in 2026, why they differ, and how to strengthen your file before you apply.

## How VA Loan Credit Score Requirements Actually Work

The VA guarantees a portion of every VA loan, which protects the lender if the loan ever defaults. Because of that guarantee, lenders can approve borrowers that a conventional loan program might reject. But each lender still sets its own internal floor, called an overlay.

In practice, most VA lenders in 2026 look for a score of about 620, while some accept 580 and a smaller group will go lower with strong compensating factors, according to [Rocket Mortgage's VA credit score guide](https://www.rocketmortgage.com/learn/va-loan-credit-score). [Veterans United](https://www.veteransunited.com/realestate/va-loans-and-credit-score-minimums-what-all-buyers-need-to-know/) reports similar ranges across the industry.

## What Lenders Review Besides Your Score

Your credit score is one input, not the whole picture. VA underwriting also weighs:

- **Payment history over the last 12 months.** Recent late payments matter more than old ones.
- **Debt-to-income ratio (DTI).** This compares your monthly debt payments to your gross income. VA guidelines flag DTI above 41 percent for closer review, though loans above that level are approved with strong residual income.
- **Residual income.** This is a VA-specific test: the money left over each month after major expenses. It is one reason VA loans perform well even for borrowers with modest scores.
- **Bankruptcy and foreclosure seasoning.** A Chapter 7 bankruptcy generally needs two years of distance; a foreclosure typically needs two years as well, which is shorter than many conventional timelines.

## What If Your Score Is Below 620?

You have three realistic paths:

- **Shop more lenders.** Overlays differ. A 590 score that fails at one lender can pass at another. A VeteranPCS lender can tell you quickly where your file stands — [connect with a VA loan expert here](https://www.veteranpcs.com/contact-agent).
- **Spend 60 to 90 days repairing the file.** Paying a maxed-out credit card below 30 percent of its limit often helps a score in one or two reporting cycles. The [Consumer Financial Protection Bureau explains what makes up a credit score](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-a-credit-score-en-315/) and how each piece is weighted.
- **Address errors.** Dispute wrong late payments or accounts that are not yours before you apply, not after.

## Why the VA Loan Is Still Worth It at Any Score

Even if your score is average, the VA loan usually beats the alternatives. There is no down payment requirement and no monthly mortgage insurance — see [how a zero-down VA loan works](/blog/how-does-a-0-down-va-loan-work) for the math. Most borrowers pay a one-time [VA funding fee](/blog/understanding-the-va-funding-fee-a-complete-guide-for-military-families) instead, and veterans receiving VA disability compensation are exempt from it, per [VA.gov's funding fee page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the minimum credit score for a VA loan?

The VA does not set one. Most lenders want about 620 in 2026, many accept 580, and some approve lower scores when income, residual income, and payment history are strong.

### Can I get a VA loan with bad credit?

Often, yes. The VA's residual income test and the government guarantee give lenders room to approve borrowers with past credit problems, especially once bankruptcies or foreclosures are two or more years behind you.

### Does checking my rate hurt my credit score?

Multiple mortgage inquiries within a short shopping window are generally scored as a single inquiry, so comparing a few VA lenders in the same two-week period has a minimal effect.

### Will a higher credit score get me a better VA rate?

Usually, yes. Lenders price loans by risk, so moving from the low 600s to 680 or above typically earns a lower rate even within the VA program.
