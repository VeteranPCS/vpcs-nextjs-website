If you have orders to one of the Air Force bases in South Carolina, you could land in a fighter town, a port city or a Guard base near the state capital. South Carolina has three main Air Force installations: Shaw Air Force Base in Sumter, Joint Base Charleston and McEntire Joint National Guard Base near Columbia. This guide covers what each base does, which town it is near, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like nearby.

## Shaw Air Force Base

Shaw AFB sits inside the Sumter city limits on more than 3,569 acres, per the [Shaw AFB fact sheet](https://www.shaw.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/663885/shaw-air-force-base/). It opened as Shaw Field on August 7, 1941. It is named for 1st Lt. Ervin David Shaw, who was killed in World War I.

Shaw's host unit is the 20th Fighter Wing. Here is what the fact sheet says about it:

- It flies the F-16 Fighting Falcon in three fighter squadrons: the 55th, 77th and 79th.
- It is the largest F-16 combat wing in the Air Force.
- It is the only "Wild Weasel" wing in the continental U.S. Wild Weasel pilots hunt and knock out enemy air defenses.
- It hosts nearly 30 other units.

Shaw is also a headquarters base. Ninth Air Force, also called U.S. Air Forces Central (AFCENT), is here. So is Third Army, also called U.S. Army Central. AFCENT is the air arm of U.S. Central Command, per the [U.S. Army Central newcomers page](https://www.usarcent.army.mil/Newcomers/).

Note that the population figures on the Shaw fact sheet date to 2013, so ask your sponsor for current numbers. For neighborhoods and commutes, read our [PCS guide to Shaw AFB and Sumter](/blog/pcs-to-shaw-afb-sumter-2026-guide).

## Joint Base Charleston

Joint Base Charleston was established on October 1, 2010. It covers about 23,000 acres in separate parcels. Per the [Joint Base Charleston snapshot](https://www.jbcharleston.jb.mil/About-Us/JB-CHS-Snapshot/), the base:

- Has about 11,500 active duty members and about 14,000 people in its total force.
- Flies 40 C-17 Globemaster III cargo jets.
- Supports more than 65 Defense Department and federal agencies.
- Serves more than 90,000 people, including families and retirees.
- Shares two runways with Charleston International Airport.

The flying unit is the 437th Airlift Wing. Its stated mission is global mobility, meaning moving troops and gear anywhere in the world, per the [437th Airlift Wing page](https://www.jbcharleston.jb.mil/Units/437th-Airlift-Wing/). It flies side by side with the 315th Airlift Wing, an Air Force Reserve unit. The 628th Air Base Wing runs the base itself.

The Navy side of Joint Base Charleston includes the Naval Information Warfare Center Atlantic. Our [PCS guide to Charleston and Joint Base Charleston](/blog/pcs-to-charleston-sc-military-family-guide-to-joint-base-charleston) covers neighborhoods like Summerville, Goose Creek and Mount Pleasant.

## McEntire Joint National Guard Base

McEntire is a 2,400-acre base about 12 miles east of Columbia, per the [169th Fighter Wing history fact sheet](https://www.169fw.ang.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Article/454063/history-of-the-south-carolina-air-national-guard/). It is home to the South Carolina Air National Guard's 169th Fighter Wing, known as the "Swamp Fox" wing.

The 169th flies the F-16. Like Shaw, it focuses on suppressing and destroying enemy air defenses. The base used to be called Congaree Air Base. It was renamed for Brig. Gen. Barnie B. McEntire Jr., the first commander of the South Carolina Air National Guard.

If you are full-time Guard staff at McEntire, the Columbia area is the natural place to look for housing. Columbia is also home to Fort Jackson. Our [PCS guide to Fort Jackson and Columbia](/blog/pcs-to-fort-jackson-columbia-2026-guide) covers housing there.

## What BAH Covers Near South Carolina Air Force Bases

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off base. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code, and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Shaw AFB and Sumter | SC263 | $1,503 / $1,290 | $1,986 / $1,491 | $2,052 / $1,785 |
| Charleston | SC259 | $2,385 / $1,941 | $2,652 / $2,223 | $2,721 / $2,529 |
| McEntire and Columbia | SC260 | $1,878 / $1,647 | $2,160 / $1,812 | $2,232 / $2,028 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 in the Sumter, Charleston and Columbia rate areas](/images/blog/air-force-bases-in-south-carolina/south-carolina-air-force-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near South Carolina's Air Force bases. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- Charleston pays the most at every grade shown. An E-5 with dependents gets $882 more a month there than in Sumter.
- Sumter pays the least of the three areas at every grade shown.
- In Sumter, an E-7 with dependents gets $495 more a month than an E-7 without dependents.

BAH is meant to cover typical rent and utilities, not your dream home. To see how your rate turns into a price range, read [how much house you can afford on BAH](/blog/how-much-house-can-you-afford-on-bah). ## Buying a Home Near South Carolina Air Force Bases

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the home's appraised value. You also do not need private mortgage insurance.

**Plan for retirement, too.** South Carolina military retirement pay is "exempt from South Carolina Individual Income Tax, no matter the recipient's age," per the [SC Department of Revenue tips for retirees](https://dor.sc.gov/tax-tips/retirees-lower-your-individual-income-tax-bill-these-five-tips). That makes the state popular with people who want to stay after they retire. Veterans with a 100% rating may also get property tax help. See our guide to [South Carolina veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/south-carolina-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026).

**Check flood risk near the coast.** Much of the Charleston area is low and close to water. Ask your agent about flood zones and flood insurance before you make an offer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Air Force bases are in South Carolina?

South Carolina has three main Air Force installations: Shaw AFB in Sumter, Joint Base Charleston and McEntire Joint National Guard Base near Columbia.

### What planes fly at Shaw AFB?

The 20th Fighter Wing at Shaw flies the F-16 Fighting Falcon. It is the largest F-16 combat wing in the Air Force.

### What aircraft are at Joint Base Charleston?

Joint Base Charleston has 40 C-17 Globemaster III cargo jets, flown by the active duty 437th Airlift Wing and the Reserve 315th Airlift Wing.

### Is Shaw AFB near Columbia?

Shaw is in Sumter. The U.S. Army Central newcomers page says Columbia is about 45 miles away, under an hour's drive.

### Which South Carolina Air Force base has the highest BAH?

Charleston. An E-5 with dependents gets $2,385 a month there in 2026, compared with $1,503 in Sumter.
