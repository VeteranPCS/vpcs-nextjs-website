If you search for Air Force bases in Colorado, you will find one surprise right away. Today only one installation in the state carries the Air Force name: the U.S. Air Force Academy. Peterson, Schriever and Buckley were Air Force bases for decades, but in 2021 they were renamed Space Force Bases.

Airmen and Guardians still serve side by side at all of them, and many Air Force members get PCS orders to these posts every year. PCS stands for Permanent Change of Station, the military's term for an assigned move. This guide covers what each installation does, where it sits, and what your housing allowance covers nearby.

## Air Force Bases in Colorado, at a Glance

| Installation | Nearest city | Host unit | Main mission |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| U.S. Air Force Academy | Colorado Springs (north) | 10th Air Base Wing | Educates and trains future Air Force and Space Force officers |
| Peterson Space Force Base | Colorado Springs | Space Base Delta 1 | Hosts NORAD, U.S. Northern Command and Space Force headquarters units |
| Schriever Space Force Base | About 10 miles east of Colorado Springs | Space Base Delta 41 | Flies the GPS satellite constellation |
| Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station | Colorado Springs | Space Base Delta 1 | NORAD and USNORTHCOM alternate command center |
| Buckley Space Force Base | East of Denver | Space Base Delta 2 | Space-based missile warning; home of the 140th Wing |

## The U.S. Air Force Academy

The Academy sits just north of Colorado Springs at the base of the Front Range. Its campus covers 18,500 acres, and more than 4,000 cadets are enrolled, per the [Academy's visitor page](https://www.usafa.edu/visitors/) and its [About page](https://www.usafa.edu/about/).

President Dwight D. Eisenhower authorized the Academy on April 1, 1954, and the cadet wing moved onto the permanent campus in late August 1958, per the [Air Force Academy fact sheet](https://www.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/104491/united-states-air-force-academy/). The 10th Air Base Wing is the host wing that runs the base itself, per the [10th Air Base Wing fact sheet](https://www.usafa.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/428281/10th-air-base-wing/).

Orders here usually mean a faculty, staff, coaching or support job. Families tend to look at northern Colorado Springs, Monument and nearby communities along Interstate 25. Our [Air Force Academy PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-air-force-academy-colorado-springs-2026-guide) covers neighborhoods and schools.

## Peterson Space Force Base

Peterson is on the east side of Colorado Springs and shares its runway with the Colorado Springs Airport. It opened as an Army airfield on April 28, 1942, became Peterson Air Force Base in 1976, and was renamed Peterson Space Force Base on July 26, 2021, per the [Peterson history fact sheet](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/1124362/peterson-space-force-base-history/).

The host unit, Space Base Delta 1, has more than 2,800 military and civilian members and supports nearly 17,000 people, per the [Space Base Delta 1 fact sheet](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/326192/space-base-delta-1/) (current as of November 2025). Its partners include:

- North American Aerospace Defense Command (NORAD) and U.S. Northern Command
- U.S. Space Command
- Space Force Combat Forces Command and Space Training and Readiness Command
- The Air Force Reserve's 302nd Airlift Wing

One change to watch: U.S. Space Command is moving its headquarters to Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville, Alabama. The move is gradual. About 10 percent of the command was working at Redstone by late summer 2026, with a goal of at least half by the end of 2028, per a [Department of War release](https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4589725/one-year-in-us-space-command-headquarters-relocation-moving-expeditiously-to-ro/) and the [Space Command newsroom](https://www.spacecom.mil/Newsroom/News/Article-Display/Article/4473792/us-space-command-takes-operational-control-of-facility-at-redstone-arsenal/). NORAD, USNORTHCOM and the Space Force units at Peterson are not part of that move. If your orders are to Space Command, ask your sponsor where your job will be. Our [Huntsville neighborhoods guide](/blog/best-neighborhoods-redstone-arsenal-military) covers the other end of that move.

## Schriever Space Force Base

Schriever sits about 10 miles east of Colorado Springs, per the [Schriever installation page](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/Schriever-SFB-Colorado/). It became Schriever Space Force Base on July 26, 2021.

Since June 18, 2025, the base has its own host unit, Space Base Delta 41, per the [Space Base Delta 41 activation story](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/Newsroom/News/Display/Article/4221169/space-base-delta-activates-at-schriever-delivers-critical-support-to-space-oper/). Schriever is best known as home of the GPS Master Control Station, where the 2nd Space Operations Squadron flies the Global Positioning System satellites, per the [Space Force GPS fact sheet](https://www.spaceforce.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Fact-Sheet-Display/Article/3743305/global-positioning-system-at-schriever-space-force-base/).

Most Schriever families live in eastern Colorado Springs, Falcon or Peyton, which keeps the drive short.

## Cheyenne Mountain Space Force Station

Cheyenne Mountain is the famous command center built inside a mountain on the southwest side of Colorado Springs. It became fully operational as the NORAD Combat Operations Center on February 6, 1967. Today it serves as the alternate command center for NORAD and USNORTHCOM and a crew training site, while day-to-day operations run from Peterson, per the [Cheyenne Mountain installation page](https://www.petersonschriever.spaceforce.mil/Cheyenne-Mountain-SFS-Colorado/).

## Buckley Space Force Base

Buckley sits east of Denver in Aurora. It became Buckley Air Force Base in 2000 and was renamed Buckley Space Force Base on June 4, 2021, per [Buckley's renaming story](https://www.buckley.spaceforce.mil/News/Article-Display/Article/2646938/buckley-receives-new-name-commander/).

The host unit, Space Base Delta 2, supports space-based missile warning, space surveillance and space communications. The base has about 3,000 active duty members from every service, 4,000 Guard and Reserve members and 2,400 civilians, per the [Space Base Delta 2 fact sheet](https://www.spaceforce.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Fact-Sheet-Display/Article/3687811/space-base-delta-2/). It also serves about 88,000 retirees, veterans and family members in the region.

Buckley is also the Air Force's flying home in the Denver area. The Colorado Air National Guard's 140th Wing flies F-16s there and stands alert to defend the Front Range, per the [140th Wing fact sheet](https://www.140wg.ang.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/444237/140th-wing-colorado-air-national-guard/). Our [Buckley PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-buckley-sfb-aurora-2026-guide) covers Aurora and the Denver suburbs.

## What BAH Covers Near Colorado's Air Force Bases

BAH is Basic Allowance for Housing, the monthly amount the military pays you toward housing when you live off base. It is based on your pay grade, whether you have dependents, and your duty station's rate area. It is not based on the rent you actually pay.

Colorado's installations fall into just two rate areas. The Academy, Peterson, Schriever and Cheyenne Mountain all use the Colorado Springs rate. Buckley uses the Denver rate. Here are 2026 monthly rates, with dependents first and without second, from the official [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/) run by the Defense Travel Management Office.

| Rate area | Installations | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Colorado Springs (CO046) | USAFA, Peterson, Schriever, Cheyenne Mountain | $2,358 / $1,860 | $2,487 / $2,166 | $2,595 / $2,397 |
| Denver (CO045) | Buckley | $2,841 / $2,148 | $3,114 / $2,568 | $3,207 / $2,979 |

![Bar chart comparing 2026 Basic Allowance for Housing with dependents in the Colorado Springs and Denver rate areas for E-5, E-7 and O-3 pay grades](/images/blog/air-force-bases-in-colorado/colorado-bah-2026.png)

*2026 BAH with dependents in Colorado's two rate areas. Source: DoD DTMO BAH rate lookup.*

An E-5 with dependents gets $483 more each month at Buckley than in Colorado Springs. That gap reflects Denver-area housing costs, so a higher allowance does not always mean more house. ## Life Near Colorado's Air Force Bases

**Altitude and weather.** Colorado Springs sits above 6,000 feet. Expect strong sun, dry air, fast-changing weather and real snow in winter, often followed by sunny days that melt it quickly.

**Two very different markets.** Colorado Springs is one large metro with many military neighborhoods, since Fort Carson is there too. Buckley families live in the bigger and pricier Denver metro.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Air Force bases are in Colorado?

Only one installation still uses the Air Force name: the U.S. Air Force Academy. Peterson, Schriever and Buckley were Air Force bases until 2021, when they became Space Force Bases. Air Force members still serve at all of them.

### Is Peterson still an Air Force base?

No. Peterson Air Force Base was renamed Peterson Space Force Base on July 26, 2021. It still hosts Air Force units, including the Air Force Reserve's 302nd Airlift Wing.

### Which Colorado base has the highest BAH?

Buckley Space Force Base, in the Denver rate area. For 2026 the with-dependents rate is $2,841 for an E-5, $3,114 for an E-7 and $3,207 for an O-3.

### Is U.S. Space Command leaving Colorado Springs?

Its headquarters is moving to Redstone Arsenal in Alabama in phases, with at least half of the command planned there by the end of 2028. NORAD, U.S. Northern Command and the Space Force units at Peterson are staying.

### Does the Air Force still fly fighters in Colorado?

Yes. The Colorado Air National Guard's 140th Wing flies F-16s from Buckley Space Force Base.
