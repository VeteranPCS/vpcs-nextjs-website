If you have orders to Naval Station Norfolk, your housing plan starts with one number: your BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing, the tax-free monthly amount the military pays you toward rent or a mortgage). Norfolk anchors the Hampton Roads region, home to the largest naval base in the world, so PCS traffic here is heavy every summer. The good news is that home prices in the area are moderate for a major coastal metro, which means your allowance can stretch. This guide lays out the real 2026 Naval Station Norfolk BAH rates and walks through a clear buy-versus-rent example so you can plan with facts instead of guesses.

For the full picture of neighborhoods, schools, and base life, start with our [PCS to Naval Station Norfolk 2026 guide](/blog/pcs-to-naval-station-norfolk-2026-guide). This post focuses on the money.

## 2026 BAH Rates for Naval Station Norfolk (MHA VA298)

BAH is set by your MHA (Military Housing Area, the geographic zone the Department of Defense uses to price rates). Naval Station Norfolk and the surrounding cities fall under MHA code VA298, the Norfolk/Portsmouth area. The table below shows the 2026 monthly rates for common ranks, effective January 1, 2026, pulled from the [Defense Travel Management Office BAH calculator](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/).

|---|---|---|
| E-4 | $2,229 | $1,707 |

| E-6 | $2,559 | $2,043 |
| E-7 | $2,604 | $2,235 |
| O-1 | $2,454 | $2,022 |
| O-2 | $2,556 | $2,367 |
| O-3 | $2,694 | $2,505 |

## What Your Allowance Buys in Hampton Roads

Norfolk is more affordable than most coastal Navy towns. The typical home value in Norfolk is about $283,005, up 3.7% over the past year, a figure Zillow calls a typical value rather than a median sale price, according to [Zillow](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/6210/norfolk-va/). Homes across the city go to pending in around 17 days, so the market moves quickly in PCS season.

On the rental side, the average rent in Norfolk is about $1,728, and a typical three-bedroom runs closer to $2,250, per [Zillow rental data](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/norfolk-va/). Property taxes are reasonable too: the City of Norfolk sets its real estate tax rate at $1.23 per $100 of assessed value, or about 1.23%, as published by the [City of Norfolk](https://www.norfolk.gov/4812/Real-Estate-Tax). Rates are set by City Council each year, so confirm the current figure before you buy.

## Buy vs. Rent: A Real Example

Let's put those numbers together for a $300,000 Norfolk-area home using a VA loan with no money down. The 30-year fixed rate averaged 6.55% the week of July 16, 2026, according to [Freddie Mac's Primary Mortgage Market Survey](https://www.freddiemac.com/pmms), so we will use that. We will also roll the VA funding fee of 2.15% into the loan, which is the common choice for a first-time user with no down payment.

- Loan amount: $306,450 ($300,000 plus the financed 2.15% funding fee)
- Principal and interest at 6.55%: about $1,947 per month
- Property taxes at 1.23%: about $308 per month
- Homeowners insurance estimate: about $150 per month
- Estimated total monthly payment: about $2,405

An E-6 with dependents receives $2,559 in BAH, which covers that estimated $2,405 payment with about $154 left over each month. Renting a comparable three-bedroom near base runs around $2,250, which is a bit less each month but builds no equity. To understand the funding fee in that math, see our [complete guide to the VA funding fee](/blog/understanding-the-va-funding-fee-a-complete-guide-for-military-families), and to see how the zero-down structure works, read [how a zero-down VA loan works](/blog/how-does-a-0-down-va-loan-work).

## When Renting Makes More Sense

Buying is not automatic. If your tour is short, if you are unsure the area fits your family, or if you expect to move again in a year or two, renting can be the smarter call because it keeps you flexible and avoids selling costs on a quick turnaround. If you do buy and later get orders, you have choices, which we cover in [rent or sell your home when you PCS](/blog/rent-or-sell-your-home-when-you-pcs). ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Does BAH cover a mortgage at Naval Station Norfolk?**
For many mid-level ranks, yes. An E-6 with dependents receives $2,559 in 2026, which covers the estimated $2,405 payment on a $300,000 home with room to spare.

**What MHA is Naval Station Norfolk in?**
It falls under MHA code VA298, the Norfolk/Portsmouth military housing area.

**Is it cheaper to rent or buy near Norfolk?**
Renting a three-bedroom runs around $2,250 a month, close to the estimated cost of buying a similar home. Buying builds equity, while renting keeps you flexible.

**How much are property taxes in Norfolk?**
The city's real estate tax rate is about $1.23 per $100 of assessed value, or roughly 1.23%, and it is set each year by City Council.
