Air Force bases in Texas are where many Airmen start their careers, from basic training to pilot training, so your next orders may well point here. Texas is home to five Air Force-run installations, including Joint Base San Antonio (JBSA), plus an Air Force Reserve fighter wing at a Navy-run base in Fort Worth. This guide covers what each base does, where it sits, and what your 2026 Basic Allowance for Housing (BAH) looks like nearby. For every branch's installations in the state, start with our hub post on [what military bases are in Texas](/blog/what-military-bases-are-in-texas).

A quick note on terms. A PCS, or Permanent Change of Station, is your official move to a new duty station. BAH is the monthly housing allowance you get when you live off base. Its amount depends on your rank, your ZIP code, and whether you have dependents.

## Joint Base San Antonio (JBSA)

Joint Base San Antonio is one joint base made up of several locations around the city. The main ones are JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Randolph, and JBSA-Fort Sam Houston, plus the Camp Bullis training area. Per the [502nd Air Base Wing fact sheet](https://www.jbsa.mil/Information/JBSA-History-Fact-Sheets/Article-View/Article/598509/502nd-air-base-wing/), the 502nd became the host unit for Lackland and Randolph in 2010 and for Fort Sam Houston later that year. In plain terms, the 502nd runs base support, while other units handle the training missions. Our [2026 JBSA PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-joint-base-san-antonio-2026-guide) covers housing, schools, and commutes in more detail.

### JBSA-Lackland

Lackland is where every enlisted Airman and Guardian starts. Per the [37th Training Wing fact sheet](https://www.37trw.af.mil/About/Fact-Sheets/Article/633018/37th-training-wing/), Basic Military Training (BMT) is the only Department of the Air Force site that trains enlisted Airmen and Guardians. The course runs about 7.5 weeks. The 37th Training Wing also runs technical schools, including all Defense Department training for military working dogs and their handlers.

### JBSA-Randolph

Randolph is the headquarters of Air Education and Training Command (AETC), the command that recruits, trains, and educates Airmen, per the [AETC fact sheet](https://www.aetc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/AETC-Fact-Sheet/). Randolph is also home to the 12th Flying Training Wing, which trains the instructors who teach new pilots.

Weighing Lackland against Randolph for a home search? [Talk with a San Antonio-area agent who works with military families](https://www.veteranpcs.com/contact-agent?form=agent&fn=Marc&id=001Rg00000dDqdR&state=texas). There is no cost and no obligation.

## Dyess AFB (Abilene)

Dyess Air Force Base (AFB) is in Abilene, in West Central Texas. Its host unit is the 7th Bomb Wing, which flies the B-1B Lancer and runs the Air Force's only B-1B formal training unit. The base also hosts the 317th Airlift Wing, which flies C-130J cargo planes.

Big changes are coming. The Air Force picked Dyess as the third home for the new B-21 Raider bomber, per the [Dyess announcement of the second and third B-21 bases](https://www.dyess.af.mil/News/Article/3904919/air-force-announces-second-and-third-b-21-raider-bases/). In 2026, the base is building new facilities to get ready while it keeps flying the B-1B. For local tips, see our [2026 Dyess AFB PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-dyess-afb-abilene-2026-guide).

## Sheppard AFB (Wichita Falls)

Sheppard AFB, in Wichita Falls, has two big missions. The host 82nd Training Wing teaches technical skills like aircraft maintenance, avionics, civil engineering, and medical jobs to students from every branch.

The second mission is Euro-NATO Joint Jet Pilot Training (ENJJPT). It is run by the 80th Flying Training Wing. Per the [ENJJPT fact sheet](https://www.sheppard.af.mil/Library/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/367537/euro-nato-joint-jet-pilot-training-program-enjjpt/), it is the world's only pilot training program staffed and managed by many nations to produce combat pilots for NATO (the North Atlantic Treaty Organization). Fourteen nations take part, and about 200 student pilots earn their wings each year. Read more in our [Wichita Falls and Sheppard AFB guide](/blog/pcs-to-wichita-falls-military-family-s-guide-to-thriving-near-sheppard-afb).

## Goodfellow AFB (San Angelo)

Goodfellow AFB in San Angelo is home to the 17th Training Wing. Per the [AETC Goodfellow AFB fact sheet](https://www.aetc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/558876/goodfellow-air-force-base/), the wing trains Air Force intelligence specialists and firefighters from all branches. Many Army, Navy, and Marine Corps intelligence troops train there too. Tours here are often short, so our [Goodfellow AFB PCS guide](/blog/pcs-to-goodfellow-afb-san-angelo-2026-guide) is a good next read.

## Laughlin AFB (Del Rio)

Laughlin AFB sits near Del Rio, close to the Mexico border. Its 47th Flying Training Wing runs Specialized Undergraduate Pilot Training, the course where new Air Force pilots earn their wings. About 400 pilots earn their wings there each year, per the [AETC Laughlin AFB fact sheet](https://www.aetc.af.mil/About-Us/Fact-Sheets/Display/Article/558878/laughlin-air-force-base/). Del Rio is a small town, so housing choices are limited. Start your search early.

## NAS JRB Fort Worth and the 301st Fighter Wing

Naval Air Station Joint Reserve Base (NAS JRB) Fort Worth is run by the Navy, but the Air Force has a big presence there. The Air Force Reserve's 301st Fighter Wing calls it home. It is the Reserve's first stand-alone F-35 unit and welcomed its first F-35A, per [Air Force Reserve Command](https://www.afrc.af.mil/News/Features/Display/Article/4021829/first-stand-alone-f-35-unit-welcomes-initial-lightning-ii/). ## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Air Force bases are in Texas?

Texas has five Air Force-run installations: Joint Base San Antonio, Dyess AFB, Sheppard AFB, Goodfellow AFB, and Laughlin AFB. JBSA includes both Lackland and Randolph. The Air Force Reserve's 301st Fighter Wing is also based at NAS JRB Fort Worth, which the Navy runs.

### Where is Air Force basic training?

All enlisted Air Force and Space Force basic training happens at JBSA-Lackland in San Antonio. The course lasts about 7.5 weeks.

### Which Texas Air Force base has the highest BAH?

For 2026, Dyess AFB in Abilene has the highest with-dependents BAH for E-7 ($2,844) and O-3 ($2,859) on our list. For E-5, Fort Worth is highest at $2,118.

### Is JBSA one base or three?

It is one joint base with several locations. JBSA-Lackland, JBSA-Randolph, and JBSA-Fort Sam Houston share one host unit, the 502nd Air Base Wing, and one BAH rate area.

### Do I have to live on base at a training base?

Not always. Your rank, family size, and base housing wait lists all play a part. Ask your gaining base's housing office early, and compare that with what your BAH can cover off base.
