California is a big state with a small Army footprint. If you have orders here, the army bases in California are easier to keep straight than you might expect, because there are only six of them and no two do the same job. One trains whole brigades for combat in the desert. One teaches foreign languages on the coast. The rest are Reserve training land and logistics sites.

This guide walks through each installation, then spends real time on the part that shapes your whole tour: what it costs to live nearby. PCS stands for Permanent Change of Station, the military's term for a move to a new duty station.

## Every Army Installation in California, at a Glance

The Army's own benefits site lists four common Army resources in the state: Fort Irwin, the Presidio of Monterey, Fort Hunter Liggett, and the Army Reserve at Camp Parks. You can see that list on the [MyArmyBenefits California resource page](https://myarmybenefits.us.army.mil/Benefit-Library/Resource-Locator/California). Two more Army-run sites round out the picture: Sierra Army Depot in the far northeast and Military Ocean Terminal Concord on San Francisco Bay.

| Installation | Where it is | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fort Irwin (National Training Center) | High Mojave Desert, about 39 miles northeast of Barstow | Large-scale combat training rotations |
| Presidio of Monterey | City of Monterey, central coast | Home of the Defense Language Institute Foreign Language Center |
| Fort Hunter Liggett | San Antonio Valley, southern Monterey County | The Army's largest Reserve installation and training area |
| Parks Reserve Forces Training Area (Camp Parks) | Dublin, east San Francisco Bay | Army Reserve garrison and West Coast training platform |
| Sierra Army Depot | Herlong, far northeastern California | Long-term storage, sustainment and repair of Army equipment |
| Military Ocean Terminal Concord | Contra Costa County, San Francisco Bay | Army seaport for ammunition and general cargo |

Only two of these are posts a family typically PCSes to: Fort Irwin and the Presidio of Monterey. The rest are Reserve sites or logistics operations with few permanent party billets.

## What Each Post Does

### Fort Irwin and the National Training Center

Fort Irwin sits in the High Mojave Desert about 39 miles northeast of Barstow, roughly midway between Las Vegas and San Bernardino. It is the Army's National Training Center, and it runs 10 large training rotations a year. Between 5,000 and 6,000 Soldiers from other installations rotate through each month, on top of a daily population of about 23,390 people, per [MilitaryINSTALLATIONS](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/in-depth-overview/fort-irwin).

The permanent party is smaller than that number suggests: about 4,601 active duty, 6,594 family members and 4,150 civilian employees. The 11th Armored Cavalry Regiment, the Blackhorse Regiment, lives here and plays the [opposing force](https://home.army.mil/irwin/units-tenants/11th-armored-cavalry-regiment) for every rotation. If you are assigned to the 11th ACR or to Operations Group, expect several days a month in the field.

Fort Irwin is remote. There are no gas stations between Barstow and the front gate, and Big Bear is about two hours away. We cover arrival details in our [PCS to Fort Irwin guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-irwin-2026-guide).

### Presidio of Monterey

The Presidio sits in the city of Monterey, about 120 miles south of San Francisco. Its main tenant is the Defense Language Institute Foreign Language Center, or DLIFLC, which teaches more than a dozen languages and is one of the largest language schools in the world.

The student body is the story here. The garrison supports about 9,692 active-duty students from all five services plus roughly 1,590 faculty and civilian staff, while only about 230 service members are permanently assigned. Most military housing is not on the Presidio itself. It sits about 8 miles away at the Ord Military Community, on part of what used to be Fort Ord, and at The Parks at Monterey Bay. Our [PCS to Presidio of Monterey guide](/blog/pcs-to-presidio-of-monterey-dli-2026-guide) covers the arrival timeline, including the four-week restriction for Soldiers coming straight from Basic Combat Training.

### Fort Hunter Liggett

Fort Hunter Liggett is in the San Antonio Valley in southern Monterey County, inland from the coast. It is the [largest Army Reserve installation](https://home.army.mil/liggett/about), with more than 160,000 acres of year-round training land used by joint, multi-component and interagency units. Permanent party is small. Most people here are units training for a few weeks at a time.

### Parks Reserve Forces Training Area (Camp Parks)

Camp Parks is in Dublin, in the east San Francisco Bay. Officially it is Parks Reserve Forces Training Area, and it is a sub-installation of Fort Hunter Liggett, about 156 miles to the south. It hosts [more than 30 tenant units](https://www.army.mil/article/287539/prfta_tenant_units_strengthen_strategic_importance_of_bay_area_army_reserve_installation), including the 91st Training Division. The site started in 1943 as a Navy staging base and the Army took it over in 1980.

### Sierra Army Depot

Sierra Army Depot is at Herlong in far northeastern California. It is one of 26 sites in the Army's Organic Industrial Base and falls under U.S. Army Tank-automotive and Armaments Command. The work is long-term storage, equipment sustainment, and repair of Army fuel and water systems, as the [TACOM depot page](https://tacom.army.mil/tacom-oib-pages/siad-oib) lays out. The workforce here is mostly civilian.

### Military Ocean Terminal Concord

Military Ocean Terminal Concord, usually called MOTCO, is on San Francisco Bay in Contra Costa County. It is the Army's California seaport for ammunition and general cargo, and one of only two Army ammunition terminals in the country. The 834th Transportation Battalion runs [three piers and an Army-owned rail system](https://www.sddc.army.mil/596th/Pages/MOTCO.aspx) there.

For the other branches in the state, see our overview of [what military bases are in California](/blog/what-military-bases-are-in-california) and our companion post on the [Air Force and Space Force side of California](/blog/air-force-bases-in-california).

## National Guard Sites Are Not Active-Duty Posts

Two more names come up often, and both are California Army National Guard sites rather than active-duty Army posts.

[Camp Roberts](https://calguard.ca.gov/cr/) spans 42,361 acres across Monterey and San Luis Obispo counties. It is the largest Army Guard training site in the state and handles armor, artillery, aviation and live-fire training.

[Joint Forces Training Base Los Alamitos](https://calguard.ca.gov/jftb_losalamitos/) is in Orange County. It is a joint training site and Southern California's emergency response hub for wildfires, earthquakes and other state missions.

## What It Costs to Live Near an Army Base in California

Here is the number that surprises people. Two Army posts in the same state can be more than $2,000 a month apart in housing allowance. BAH stands for Basic Allowance for Housing, the monthly amount the military pays you toward off-post housing. It is set by rank, dependent status and the ZIP code of your duty station, not by the state you live in.

Fort Irwin sits in the Barstow and Fort Irwin housing area (CA028), ZIP 92310. The Presidio of Monterey sits in the Monterey housing area (CA039), ZIP 93944. These are the 2026 monthly rates from the official [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/).

| Pay grade | Fort Irwin, with dependents | Fort Irwin, without | Presidio, with dependents | Presidio, without |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E-5 | $2,001 | $1,695 | $3,465 | $2,844 |
| E-7 | $2,547 | $1,911 | $4,218 | $3,258 |
| O-3 | $2,748 | $2,274 | $4,824 | $3,723 |

An O-3 with dependents draws $2,076 more a month at Monterey than at Fort Irwin. That gap is not a raise. It reflects what local housing actually costs. MilitaryINSTALLATIONS notes that the cost of living near Fort Irwin runs about 15 percent below the national average, while Monterey-area housing is among the most expensive in the country, with local rentals running from about $2,856 for a one-bedroom apartment to $5,200 for a house.

## On Post, Renting, or Buying

You have three options, and the right one depends on which post you are headed to.

On post, you give up your BAH and your rent is covered. That trade is predictable, which is why it is the common first move. At Fort Irwin you visit Family Housing in Building 4553 Tippecanoe to get on the waitlist. At the Presidio, move-ins are described as quick, so get on the list as soon as you have orders.

Renting off post buys you neighborhood and school choice. Near Fort Irwin that usually means Barstow or Victorville and a long drive across the desert. Near Monterey it means competing in a tight coastal market where many landlords do not accept pets.

Buying is the biggest decision, and BAH is the ceiling on a comfortable payment, not the target. Taxes, insurance and utilities all take a bite. Our walkthrough on [how much house you can afford on BAH](/blog/how-much-house-can-you-afford-on-bah) shows the math, and veterans buying here should read up on [California veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/california-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026) before closing.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Army bases are in California?

Six Army-run installations, counting active-duty posts, Reserve garrisons and logistics sites: Fort Irwin, the Presidio of Monterey, Fort Hunter Liggett, Camp Parks, Sierra Army Depot and Military Ocean Terminal Concord. Camp Roberts and Joint Forces Training Base Los Alamitos are California Army National Guard sites, not active-duty Army posts.

### Which is the biggest Army base in California?

It depends how you measure. Fort Irwin has by far the largest population, with a daily count around 23,390. By land, Fort Hunter Liggett is bigger at more than 160,000 acres, but it is a Reserve training site with few permanent residents.

### Is Fort Ord still an Army base?

No. Fort Ord closed as an active installation, but part of the old property is now the Ord Military Community, which provides housing for people assigned to the Presidio of Monterey and the Naval Postgraduate School.

### Can I live off post at Fort Irwin or the Presidio of Monterey?

Yes, at both. You keep your BAH and rent or buy on the local market, but check in with the housing office first. At Fort Irwin the practical limit is the drive, since the nearest town is about 39 miles away. At Monterey the limit is price and supply.

### What is BAH at Fort Irwin and the Presidio of Monterey for 2026?

For 2026, the with-dependents rate at Fort Irwin is $2,001 for an E-5, $2,547 for an E-7 and $2,748 for an O-3. At the Presidio of Monterey it is $3,465, $4,218 and $4,824 for those same grades. Confirm your own pay grade in the DTMO BAH rate lookup linked above, because rates reset every January.
