_Updated October 2026_

Myrtle Beach military retirement is a real option worth a close look. South Carolina does not tax military retirement pay, the Grand Strand has a large VA clinic and a Vet Center, and the area keeps its military history in plain view. Add the beach, state parks, and an airport with nonstop flights to dozens of cities, and it is easy to see why many veterans settle here after their last move.

This guide has two voices. Michael Sheehan is a retired service member and Myrtle Beach real estate agent who moved here with his wife and two daughters about three years ago. His personal story is clearly marked below. The tax rules, benefits, and facility details were researched and checked by VeteranPCS against state and VA sources.

## The Short Answer: Why Military Retirees Choose Myrtle Beach

- South Carolina lets residents deduct all qualifying military retirement income from state income tax, with no age limit.
- Veterans the VA rates as totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected condition may qualify for a full property tax exemption on their home.
- The Myrtle Beach VA Clinic offers primary care, mental health care, and specialty care close to home.
- Veteran groups, Military Appreciation Days each May, and Warbird Park keep service visible in daily life.
- Beaches, state parks, golf, shows, and restaurants mean visiting family always has something to do.

## South Carolina Military Retirement Tax Rules

Michael's advice for anyone comparing places to retire is to look past home prices and at the long-term money picture. State taxes are a big part of that.

### Military retirement pay is fully deductible

According to [South Carolina Department of Revenue Revenue Ruling 22-11](https://dor.sc.gov/sites/dor/files/policies/RR22-11.pdf), starting with tax year 2022, South Carolina residents may deduct all military retirement income that is included in their state taxable income. There is no minimum age. The deduction covers retirement pay from active duty and from the National Guard or Reserve components.

A few details matter:

- The deduction applies to income from a qualified military retirement plan. Civilian federal pensions do not count under this rule.
- A surviving spouse who receives military retirement income from a deceased spouse can use the deduction the same way the service member could.
- This is a state rule only. Federal income tax still applies to retirement pay.
- You get the deduction as a South Carolina resident, so where you claim legal residence matters.

If you also work after retiring, wages are taxed like anyone else's. Talk with a tax professional about your full situation before you decide.

### Property tax exemption for disabled veterans

South Carolina also offers a strong property tax break, but it is **not** for every veteran. Per the [South Carolina Department of Revenue guide to veteran property tax exemptions](https://dor.sc.gov/tax-index/property/exempt-property/Documents/tax-tips-for-veterans-rack-card-2024.pdf), it is for veterans the VA has rated totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected condition. "Service-connected" means the VA found the condition is linked to your military service.

If you qualify, the exemption can cover:

- Your home and up to five acres of land, when it is your legal residence.
- Up to two private passenger vehicles owned or leased by you, or jointly with a spouse who lives with you.

Surviving spouses may keep a home and vehicle exemption if they meet the state's rules. You can apply online through the state's MyDORWAY portal or by mailing form PT-401-I. You will need your VA disability letter, your recorded deed, and proof of legal residence from the county assessor.

Retirees who do not meet that standard may still benefit from South Carolina's separate homestead exemption for residents who are 65 or older, totally disabled, or legally blind. ## VA Health Care and Veteran Support on the Grand Strand

### Myrtle Beach VA Clinic

The [Myrtle Beach VA Clinic](https://www.va.gov/charleston-health-care/locations/myrtle-beach-va-clinic/) is at 1800 Airpark Drive. It is part of the Ralph H. Johnson VA Health Care System, which is based in Charleston. The VA lists primary care, mental health care, geriatrics, lab work, and imaging services, including MRI and CT.

When the clinic opened in April 2021, the VA said the [84,000-square-foot building](https://www.va.gov/charleston-health-care/stories/new-myrtle-beach-va-outpatient-clinic-opens/) added specialty care such as cardiology, physical therapy, audiology, and optometry. It also brought prosthetics and MRI services to the Myrtle Beach area for the first time.

### Myrtle Beach Vet Center

The [Myrtle Beach Vet Center](https://www.va.gov/myrtle-beach-vet-center/) at 1101 Johnson Avenue offers free, confidential counseling in a non-medical setting. Services include individual, group, couples, and family counseling, along with help during the move to civilian life. Eligibility depends on factors such as combat service or military sexual trauma, but you do not need to be enrolled in VA health care or have a disability rating to use it. Family members of eligible veterans can get help too.

### Veteran organizations

Michael is a member of [DAV Carroll E. Prosser Chapter 30](https://davwebsites.dav.org/sc/30/SystemPages/Home.aspx) in Myrtle Beach. DAV stands for Disabled American Veterans. The chapter holds claims service sessions to help veterans with VA benefit claims. In his words, he has "seen firsthand the role organizations like DAV can play in connecting veterans with resources and with one another."

VFW (Veterans of Foreign Wars) posts are active across the area too, including [VFW Post 10420 in Murrells Inlet](https://www.vfwpost10420.org/). Check each group's site for current meeting times.

If you have a VA disability rating, it can also help when you buy. The VA [waives the VA loan funding fee](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/funding-fee-and-closing-costs/) for veterans who receive VA compensation for a service-connected disability. Our guide to [using VA disability income to qualify for a mortgage](/blog/using-va-disability-income-to-qualify-for-a-mortgage) explains how lenders count that income.

## Military History You Can See and Visit

### Military Appreciation Days each May

Myrtle Beach holds Military Appreciation Days around Memorial Day weekend. The [City of Myrtle Beach](https://www.cityofmyrtlebeach.com/news_detail_T6_R2332.php) lists a parade at The Market Common, the Jack Platt Veterans' March and Battlefield Cross Ceremony on Ocean Boulevard, and a Memorial Day remembrance ceremony. The 2026 schedule, as [published by Tourism Works for the Grand Strand](https://www.tourismworksforthegrandstrand.com/tourism-news/memorialdaycelebrations2026/), also included a Vietnam memorial dedication at Warbird Park. Dates and locations change each year, so check the city's calendar in the spring.

### The Market Common and Warbird Park

The Market Common shopping and residential district sits on land that was once Myrtle Beach Air Force Base. According to [Warbird Park](https://warbirdpark.com/index.html), the base was home to the 354th Tactical Fighter Wing and was deactivated in March 1993. The park now displays former military aircraft, including an A-10, and memorials to World War II, Korea, POWs, 9/11, and Purple Heart recipients. Admission is free from sunrise to sunset.

For Michael, this is part of the appeal. In his words, "it's meaningful to live somewhere where service and sacrifice continue to be recognized by the community."

## Michael's Story: Why Myrtle Beach Became Home

*This section reflects Michael Sheehan's personal experience. It is not a promise about any specific home or neighborhood.*

Michael and his family chose Myrtle Beach after his military career. One thing he values is the mix of people at different stages of life. He meets young families building their lives, retirees starting their next chapter, and grandparents spending time with family.

The lifestyle is what made it feel like home. His family found a house close enough to walk to the beach and watch the sunrise. As he puts it, they can enjoy the beach "as often as we want, rather than having to plan an entire vacation around it."

After three years, his favorite moments are simple: walking to the beach with his wife and daughters, watching the sun come up over the Atlantic, and realizing that what used to feel like a vacation spot "is now simply home."

## Outdoor Life Beyond the Beach

The Grand Strand is the long stretch of South Carolina coast centered on Myrtle Beach. It is more than sand and ocean. Michael points to the forests, state parks, and trails that make for an easy morning outside.

- [Myrtle Beach State Park](https://southcarolinaparks.com/myrtle-beach) sits in an oceanfront maritime forest, with a nature trail, a fishing pier, and campsites.
- Huntington Beach State Park, south near Murrells Inlet, is another popular spot for walking and wildlife.
- Fishing, boating, biking, and golf are available across the area.

## Things to Do When Family Visits

Myrtle Beach is built around hospitality, so visitors rarely run out of plans. Michael says his family enjoys playing tourist when people come to town. One day can be a beach day, the next a walk at a state park, and the next dinner and a live show.

Getting family here is easier, too. [Myrtle Beach International Airport](https://www.flymyrtlebeach.com/flight-information/route-map/) advertises nonstop service to more than 50 destinations on 10 airlines. Some routes may be seasonal, so check current schedules if you plan to fly often.

### A local tradition: the Carolina Shag

Michael's favorite example of local culture is the Shag, a partner dance tied to the beach music scene of North Myrtle Beach and the Grand Strand. The [South Carolina Legislature](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/studentpage/coolstuff/music.shtml) made the Shag the official state dance in 1984.

## Things to Weigh Before You Buy

Every place has tradeoffs. A good plan covers these:

- **Flood and wind risk.** Coastal homes can carry higher insurance costs. Check a home's flood zone on the [FEMA Flood Map Service Center](https://msc.fema.gov/portal/home) and get insurance quotes before you make an offer.
- **Tourist seasons.** Traffic and crowds rise in summer. Visit in peak and off-peak months if you can.
- **Distance to care.** Many VA specialty services are local, but some care may still route to Charleston. Ask your VA care team.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does South Carolina tax military retirement pay?

No. Starting with tax year 2022, South Carolina residents can deduct all qualifying military retirement income from state taxable income, with no age requirement. Federal income tax still applies.

### Do all veterans get a property tax exemption in South Carolina?

No. The full home and vehicle exemption is for veterans the VA rates as totally and permanently disabled from a service-connected condition, plus certain surviving spouses. Other retirees may qualify for the separate homestead exemption at age 65.

### Is there a VA clinic in Myrtle Beach?

Yes. The Myrtle Beach VA Clinic at 1800 Airpark Drive offers primary care, mental health care, imaging, and specialty services. The Myrtle Beach Vet Center offers free, confidential counseling.

### When are Military Appreciation Days in Myrtle Beach?

They are held around Memorial Day weekend each May. Events have included a parade at The Market Common, a veterans' march, a Battlefield Cross Ceremony, and a Memorial Day ceremony.
