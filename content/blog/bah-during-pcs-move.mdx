BAH during a PCS move confuses a lot of service members, and the stakes are real. BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) is often the second-largest part of your pay, and a PCS (Permanent Change of Station) changes it. The short version: you keep your old duty station's rate while you travel, and the new rate starts when you sign in at your new base. This guide walks through each stage of the move, plus advance BAH, rate protection and what happens if your family stays behind.

Rules differ a bit by service and situation, so always confirm your own case with your finance office. Think of this as the map, not the final word.

## BAH During a PCS: The Timeline

![Four-step timeline showing how BAH changes from orders, to leaving the old base, to signing in at the new base, to settling in](/images/blog/bah-during-pcs-move/bah-pcs-timeline.png)
*How BAH usually changes during a move within the United States. Sources: DoD Financial Management Regulation Vol. 7A, Ch. 26; DTMO; Army and Air Force finance briefs.*

### Step 1: You get orders

Your BAH does not change when orders arrive. This is the time to plan your housing budget. Look up the rate at your new duty station with the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). You will need the ZIP code of the new base, your pay grade, and whether you have dependents.

If you need cash for a new lease, you can ask about advance BAH (more on that below). Our guide to [PCS advance pay](/blog/pcs-advance-pay-guide) covers the other advances you can request.

### Step 2: You leave your old base and travel

While you travel, BAH stays tied to your old duty station. An Army finance brief from Fort Lee puts it plainly: BAH while in transit is based on your losing Permanent Duty Station (PDS), per the [Fort Lee out-processing brief](https://home.army.mil/lee/2717/9070/4207/Out-Processing-Brief-20260923.pdf) (September 2026).

There are exceptions. Members without dependents who lived in the barracks or dorms, and members coming from overseas, usually get the in-transit rate, called BAH-T, until they sign in at the new base, per a [Luke AFB PCS travel entitlements handout](https://www.luke.af.mil/Portals/58/PCS%20Travel%20Entitlements%20Handout.pdf) (2025). BAH-T is a flat rate that does not depend on location, per the [DTMO BAH page](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/).

Your travel pay, such as mileage and per diem, is separate from BAH. See our guide to [PCS travel pay, mileage and per diem](/blog/pcs-travel-pay-mileage-per-diem-2026).

### Step 3: You sign in at your new base

The new rate starts when you report for duty, not when you arrive in town or sign a lease. The DoD's pay rules say eligibility at the new PDS "starts on the Service member's reporting day," per the [DoD Financial Management Regulation, Volume 7A, Chapter 26](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/documents/fmr/current/07a/07a_26.pdf). Some finance offices describe it as the day after you sign in, so ask yours how they process it.

This matters for timing. If the new base pays more, signing in on time gets you the higher rate sooner. If it pays less, budget for the drop before you sign a lease.

### Step 4: You settle in

Once you are on the new rate, a few rules shape what you get next:

- **Rate protection.** If BAH rates drop at your location, you keep the higher rate as long as your status does not change. A PCS is one of the changes that ends protection, per the [DTMO BAH page](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/). After you arrive, you get the same protection at the new location.
- **On-base housing.** If you move into government family housing, BAH generally stops once quarters are assigned, per the [DoD Financial Management Regulation, Chapter 26](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/documents/fmr/current/07a/07a_26.pdf).
- **Temporary lodging.** While you look for a home, Temporary Lodging Expense (TLE) can help pay for a hotel. For moves within the U.S., TLE now covers up to 21 days at up to $290 a day, per [MARADMIN 111/25](https://www.marines.mil/News/Messages/Messages-Display/Article/4115495/temporary-lodging-expense-period-increase/). Our [TLE guide](/blog/temporary-lodging-expense-tle-pcs-guide) explains how to claim it.

Need help finding a home that fits your new rate? [Connect with a VeteranPCS agent near your next base](https://www.veteranpcs.com/contact-agent). There is no cost and no obligation.

## Advance BAH for a New Lease

Advance BAH helps you pay move-in costs like a security deposit and first month's rent at your new duty station. Per the [DoD Financial Management Regulation, Chapter 26](https://comptroller.war.gov/Portals/45/documents/fmr/current/07a/07a_26.pdf):

- You can get up to 3 months of your expected BAH.
- You usually repay it over 12 months, and the repayment can stretch in some cases.
- You should request it within 30 days of the expense.

If your landlord returns your deposit, you pay that part back right away. Advance BAH is for renting, not for buying a home, per the [Navy's advance BAH request form](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Reference/Forms/NPPSC/NPPSC7000-2_REV_04-2024.pdf). If you need to break your current lease to move, read our guide to [breaking a lease with military orders under the SCRA](/blog/breaking-a-lease-with-military-orders-scra).

## When Your Family Stays Behind

Sometimes the family cannot move right away. A spouse may have a job, or a child may be finishing the school year. You may still be able to get BAH at a rate that fits where your family lives.

![Table of options when dependents stay behind during a PCS, including the family stability rule, Navy old-station BAH, the secretarial process, and overseas tours](/images/blog/bah-during-pcs-move/bah-dependents-stay-behind.png)
*Ways BAH can follow your dependents. Sources: 37 U.S.C. 403a; NAVADMIN 192/24; DoD FMR Vol. 7A, Ch. 26; DTMO.*

**The family stability rule.** Federal law, [37 U.S.C. 403a](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/37/403a), covers moves within the U.S. It applies from 180 days before to 180 days after your PCS. You may qualify if your spouse is working or in school, a child is in grades K-12, a family member is enrolled in the Exceptional Family Member Program (EFMP), or a family member has a chronic illness. Your service can then pay BAH at the new duty station rate, the old duty station rate, or your dependents' location rate.

**Navy old-station BAH.** Since October 2024, Sailors can ask to keep their old duty station rate while dependents stay near the old base, per the [NAVADMIN 192/24 fact sheet](https://www.mynavyhr.navy.mil/Portals/55/Messages/NAVADMIN/FACT_SHEETS/Fact_Sheet_NAV_192_24.pdf). You get only one housing allowance, and it ends if the family moves.

**Separate households.** In other cases, your service may approve a separate household through what the DoD calls the secretarial process. Ask your finance office or command early, since approvals take time.

## BAH and Overseas Moves

If you PCS overseas, BAH is replaced by OHA (Overseas Housing Allowance). OHA repays your actual rent up to a cap, per the [DTMO OHA page](https://travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Overseas-Housing-Allowance). Our [OHA guide](/blog/overseas-housing-allowance-oha-guide) covers how it works, and our guide to [what to do with your home when you PCS overseas](/blog/pcsing-oconus-what-do-i-do-with-my-home) helps with the house you leave behind.

## When 2027 BAH Rates Come Out

New BAH rates take effect each January 1. The 2026 rates were released on December 11, 2025, with an average increase of 4.2 percent, per the [Joint Base San Antonio repost of the DoD's 2026 BAH release](https://www.jbsa.mil/News/News/Article/4358569/department-of-war-releases-2026-basic-allowance-for-housing-rates/). As of November 2026, the 2027 rates had not been announced. Based on past years, expect them in mid-December. ## Turning Your New BAH Into a Housing Plan

Once you know your new rate, decide whether to rent or buy. BAH is tax-free and meant to cover typical rent and utilities in your area. Many military families use it toward a mortgage with a VA loan, which lets eligible buyers purchase with no down payment, per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/). To see what your rate could buy, read [how much house you can afford on BAH](/blog/how-much-house-can-you-afford-on-bah).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What BAH rate do I get while traveling during a PCS?

Most members keep the BAH rate of their old duty station while they travel. Members without dependents who lived in barracks, and members coming from overseas, usually get the in-transit rate (BAH-T) until they sign in.

### When does my BAH change to the new duty station rate?

When you sign in for duty at the new base. It does not change when you arrive in the area or sign a lease. Ask your finance office exactly how they record the date.

### Does rate protection carry over after a PCS?

No. A PCS ends rate protection at your old location. Once you are on the new location's rate, you are protected from future decreases there.

### Can I keep BAH at my old location if my family stays?

Possibly. For moves within the U.S., 37 U.S.C. 403a lets your service pay the old location rate in some cases, such as a spouse's job or a child in school. The Navy also has a specific old-station BAH request.

### How much advance BAH can I get?

Up to 3 months of your expected BAH, usually repaid over 12 months. It is meant for rental move-in costs, not a home purchase.
