If you have orders to Fort Bragg, your housing budget starts with one number: your BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing, the tax-free monthly amount the military pays you toward rent or a mortgage). The good news for Fayetteville families is that this is one of the more affordable markets around a major installation. Home prices are moderate, the county just cut its property tax rate, and your allowance tends to stretch. This guide lays out the real 2026 Fort Bragg BAH rates and walks through a clear buy-versus-rent example so you can plan with facts instead of guesses.

A quick note on the name: the post returned to Fort Bragg in 2025 after a short period as Fort Liberty, and it now honors Private First Class Roland L. Bragg, a World War II soldier. As the home of the XVIII Airborne Corps, the 82nd Airborne Division, and Army Special Operations, Bragg supports tens of thousands of service members and their families. For the full picture of life in the area, start with our [PCS to Fort Bragg 2026 guide](/blog/pcs-to-fort-bragg-2026-guide).

## 2026 BAH Rates for Fort Bragg (MHA NC182)

BAH is set by your MHA (Military Housing Area, the geographic zone the Department of Defense uses to price rates). Fort Bragg and Pope Army Airfield fall under MHA code NC182. The table below shows the 2026 monthly rates for common ranks, effective January 1, 2026.

| Rank | With Dependents | Without Dependents |
|---|---|---|
| E-4 | $1,722 | $1,341 |
| E-5 | $1,806 | $1,527 |
| E-6 | $2,049 | $1,638 |
| E-7 | $2,094 | $1,722 |
| O-1 | $1,842 | $1,635 |
| O-2 | $2,046 | $1,782 |
| O-3 | $2,175 | $1,956 |

*Source: 2026 rates for MHA NC182, effective January 1, 2026, per the [Department of Defense BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). The table above covers select ranks only.*

Rates for the Fort Bragg area rose modestly for 2026. Even if your local rate ever fell, rate protection means current members at the same rank and dependency status do not see a decrease; your number only changes when you move, get promoted, or change dependency status. ## Why Your BAH Goes Further Near Fort Bragg

Two things help a dollar of BAH stretch here.

First, BAH is tax-free. The amount on your Leave and Earnings Statement is not taxed as income, so a $2,049 allowance is worth more than $2,049 of taxable salary. When you compare BAH to a mortgage payment, you are comparing tax-free money to a real bill, which is a favorable trade.

Second, Fayetteville home prices are low for a major-base market. The typical home value in the city of Fayetteville is about $199,246, per [Zillow's Fayetteville home values page](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/52001/fayetteville-nc/) (a typical-value measure, not a median sale price). Homes closer to the base or in newer suburbs can run higher, but a service member's allowance still covers a large share of a typical payment.

Property taxes are moderate and recently dropped. After a 2025 revaluation, Cumberland County set its rate at 49.9 cents per $100 of value for the 2026 fiscal year, per the [Cumberland County tax rates page](https://www.cumberlandcountync.gov/departments/tax-group/tax/tax-rates). Homes inside the City of Fayetteville add a municipal rate of about 44.95 cents per $100, so an in-city owner pays a combined rate near 0.95 percent, while a home in the unincorporated county owes less. North Carolina also offers a property tax exclusion for qualifying disabled veterans; see our [North Carolina veteran property tax guide](/blog/north-carolina-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026) to check whether you qualify.

## Buy vs. Rent: A 2026 Example

Let's build a rough example so you can see the math. Every input is stated below. These are estimates, not a quote, and your real numbers will depend on your lender, your address, and your exact rate.

Here are the assumptions:

- Home price: $200,000, anchored to the typical Fayetteville home value above.
- Loan: a $0 down VA (Department of Veterans Affairs) loan, 30-year fixed. See our explainer on [how a $0 down VA loan works](/blog/how-does-a-0-down-va-loan-work).
- Interest rate: about 6.6 percent, the conservative national average for a 30-year fixed VA purchase loan in early July 2026 per [Bankrate's VA loan rates page](https://www.bankrate.com/mortgages/va-loan-rates/). Rates vary by lender; a best-case quote sat near 5.75 percent as of July 9, 2026, per [Veterans United's VA mortgage rates page](https://www.veteransunited.com/va-loans/va-mortgage-rates/). The VA does not set rates.

Now the arithmetic. On a $200,000 loan at 6.6 percent for 30 years, the estimated monthly principal and interest is about $1,277. To that we add the other parts of PITI (principal, interest, taxes, and insurance):

- Property taxes: at a combined in-city rate near 0.95 percent, that is about $1,900 a year, or roughly $158 a month.
- Homeowner's insurance: a reasonable estimate is about $140 a month, though your real quote may differ.

Adding those gives an estimated monthly housing cost of about $1,575 ($1,277 + $158 + $140). Compare that to the 2026 Fort Bragg BAH with dependents for an E-5 ($1,806), E-6 ($2,049), or E-7 ($2,094). In each case, the tax-free allowance covers the full estimated payment with room to spare, and even an E-4 with dependents ($1,722) clears it.

## When Renting Still Makes Sense

Buying is not the right call for everyone. Renting often makes more sense in a few situations.

A short tour is the biggest one. If you expect to be at Bragg for only a year or two, the closing costs of buying and selling can outweigh the equity you build in that time. Renting keeps you flexible. It also makes sense if your follow-on assignment is uncertain, if you are still learning which Fayetteville-area neighborhoods fit your family, or if you simply do not want the upkeep of a home while juggling a demanding job.

Local rents are reasonable enough that renting is a comfortable choice while you get your footing. The point of the example above is not to push you toward buying. It is to show that, in this market, buying is genuinely competitive with renting once you account for tax-free BAH and moderate property taxes. One thing worth checking first: the VA loan does not automatically carry the lowest rate for every buyer, so compare offers. See our explainer on whether [the VA loan always has the best rate](/blog/does-the-va-loan-always-have-the-best-rate).

## The Bottom Line

Fort Bragg and Pope sit under MHA NC182, and 2026 BAH runs from $1,722 for an E-4 with dependents up to $2,175 for an O-3 with dependents. Because BAH is tax-free and Fayetteville home prices and property taxes are moderate, that allowance stretches further here than in most base markets. In our example, an E-5, E-6, or E-7 with dependents could cover a typical Fayetteville home's estimated payment with BAH alone. Whether you buy or rent comes down to your timeline, your stability, and the home you find. The smartest first move is to confirm your exact rate with the official calculator, then run real numbers with someone who knows the local market.
