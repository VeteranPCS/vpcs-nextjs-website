Most of the Army bases in Colorado come down to one name: Fort Carson. "The Mountain Post" sits just south of Colorado Springs and is home to tens of thousands of Soldiers and family members. But the Army in Colorado also includes a huge training area in the southeast, a Guard missile defense unit, and a chemical depot that just finished its last mission. This guide covers each site, the towns nearby, and what 2026 BAH (Basic Allowance for Housing) looks like.

## Fort Carson: The Mountain Post

Fort Carson is big. Per the [Fort Carson Fact Sheet from February 2026](https://home.army.mil/carson/9517/7135/7055/Fort_Carson_Fact_Sheet_Feb_2026.pdf), the post covers 137,403 acres. Its total population is 89,630 people. That includes:

- 23,716 active duty Soldiers.
- 57,220 family members.
- 5,412 civilian employees.
- 1,450 contractors.

Here is the number that matters most if you are house hunting: **82% of Fort Carson Soldiers and families live off post**, per the same fact sheet. That means most families rent or buy in Colorado Springs, Fountain and nearby towns. The fact sheet also puts the post's yearly economic impact at more than $2.5 billion.

### Who Is at Fort Carson?

The biggest unit is the 4th Infantry Division, known as the "Ivy Division." The Army is changing the division to Stryker brigades. A Stryker is an eight-wheeled armored vehicle. Per a [July 2026 army.mil article](https://www.army.mil/article/293975/iron_brigade_trades_tanks_for_strykers_supports_army_transformation), the 3rd Armored Brigade traded its tanks for Strykers this summer. The division also has a combat aviation brigade, a sustainment brigade and division artillery.

Other units on the [Fort Carson tenant list](https://home.army.mil/carson/units-tenants/tenants) include:

- 10th Special Forces Group (Airborne).
- 71st Ordnance Group, which handles explosive ordnance disposal (EOD).
- 1st Space Brigade, part of Army Space and Missile Defense Command.
- 627th Hospital Center.
- The Army's World Class Athlete Program.

Unit changes can affect when people move and how long they stay. If your unit is changing, ask your sponsor about timing before you sign a lease or buy.

## Piñon Canyon Maneuver Site

Fort Carson units do much of their large field training far from the main post. Piñon Canyon Maneuver Site is in Las Animas County in southeast Colorado, between La Junta and Trinidad. Per the Fort Carson Fact Sheet, it covers about 235,000 acres. That is bigger than Fort Carson itself.

No one lives at Piñon Canyon. But if your Soldier trains there, expect weeks of time away from home. That matters when you choose a home. Some families pick a neighborhood close to friends, schools and support, not just close to the gate.

## The 100th Missile Defense Brigade

Not every Army unit in Colorado is at Fort Carson. The 100th Missile Defense Brigade is a Colorado Army National Guard unit. Per this [army.mil article on the brigade](https://www.army.mil/article/270375/100th_missile_defense_brigade_defending_america_for_20_years), it is headquartered in Colorado Springs and runs its mission from Schriever Space Force Base. The brigade operates the nation's ground-based missile defense system. It has a battalion at Fort Greely, Alaska.

The Colorado Army National Guard has nearly 4,000 Soldiers, per its [Joint Force Headquarters page](https://co.ng.mil/Army/Joint-Force-HQ/). The headquarters is in Centennial, south of Denver.

## Pueblo Chemical Depot

For decades, the Pueblo Chemical Depot stored chemical weapons. That mission is now done. Per the [Defense Threat Reduction Agency](https://www.dtra.mil/News-Media/Newsroom/Article/3446392/completion-of-chemical-agent-destruction-at-the-pueblo-chemical-agent-destructi), the last chemical munition at Pueblo was destroyed on June 22, 2023. That eliminated more than 2,613 tons of agent.

Per [army.mil](https://www.army.mil/article/280066/pueblo_chemical_depot_deactivation_ends_82_year_legacy), the depot was deactivated on September 12, 2024. The plant is being cleaned up and torn down or reused. The land will go to a local group for new uses. Pueblo is not a duty station for most families, but it is still an affordable place to live in southern Colorado.

## What BAH Covers Near Colorado Army Sites

BAH is a monthly, tax-free payment to help cover housing off base. It depends on your pay grade, your duty ZIP code and whether you have dependents. Here are 2026 rates from the [DTMO BAH rate lookup](https://www.travel.dod.mil/Allowances/Basic-Allowance-for-Housing/BAH-Rate-Lookup/). Each cell shows with dependents first, then without.

| Location | Rate area | E-5 | E-7 | O-3 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Fort Carson and Colorado Springs | CO046 | $2,358 / $1,860 | $2,487 / $2,166 | $2,595 / $2,397 |
| Pueblo | ZZ780 | $1,815 / $1,476 | $2,208 / $1,692 | $2,346 / $2,013 |

![Grouped bar chart comparing 2026 with-dependents BAH for E-5, E-7 and O-3 in the Colorado Springs and Pueblo rate areas](/images/blog/army-bases-in-colorado/colorado-army-bah-2026.png)
*2026 monthly BAH with dependents near Colorado's Army sites. Source: Defense Travel Management Office BAH rate lookup.*

A few things stand out:

- Your BAH is set by your duty station, not where you live. A Fort Carson Soldier gets the Colorado Springs rate even if the family lives in Pueblo.
- In Colorado Springs, an E-5 with dependents gets $498 more a month than an E-5 without dependents.
- The jump from E-5 to O-3 with dependents is only $237 a month in Colorado Springs.

## The Colorado Springs Housing Market

Per [Zillow's Colorado Springs home values page](https://www.zillow.com/home-values/4172/colorado-springs-co/), the typical home value in the city was about $435,351 as of October 31, 2026. That is a typical value (Zillow Home Value Index), not a median sale price. Values were down 1.8% from a year earlier.

A slightly cooler market can help buyers. Sellers may be more open to paying some closing costs or fixing repair items. To see how your BAH turns into a price range, read [how much house you can afford on BAH](/blog/how-much-house-can-you-afford-on-bah).

## Buying a Home Near Fort Carson

**Use your VA loan.** A VA loan is a home loan backed by the Department of Veterans Affairs. Per the [VA's purchase loan page](https://www.va.gov/housing-assistance/home-loans/loan-types/purchase-loan/), you can buy with no down payment as long as the price is not higher than the appraised value. There is no private mortgage insurance.

**Know the schools.** Per the [Military OneSource Fort Carson education page](https://installations.militaryonesource.mil/military-installation/fort-carson/education/education), Fountain-Fort Carson School District 8 runs the schools on post. Fountain-Fort Carson High School is off post in Fountain. Colorado also lets families apply to schools in other districts.

**Know the tax breaks.** Veterans with a 100% permanent service-connected disability may exempt 50% of the first $200,000 of their home's actual value from property tax, per the [Colorado Division of Property Taxation](https://dpt.colorado.gov/property-tax-exemption-for-veterans-with-a-disability-and-gold-star-spouses). The deadline to apply with the county assessor is July 1. See our guide to [Colorado veteran property tax exemptions](/blog/colorado-veteran-property-tax-exemptions-2026). Retirees under 55 can subtract up to $15,000 of military retirement pay from state taxable income each year through 2028, per the [Colorado Department of Revenue](https://tax.colorado.gov/retired-servicemembers).

If Space Force or Air Force orders are also on the table, read [Air Force bases in Colorado](/blog/air-force-bases-in-colorado) and our [guide to Peterson and Schriever Space Force Bases](/blog/pcs-to-peterson-schriever-sfb-colorado-springs-2026-guide).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How many Army bases are in Colorado?

Fort Carson is the only active duty Army post in Colorado. The Army also runs the Piñon Canyon Maneuver Site for training. The Colorado Army National Guard's 100th Missile Defense Brigade works from Schriever Space Force Base.

### What division is at Fort Carson?

The 4th Infantry Division, known as the Ivy Division. It is moving to Stryker brigades. Fort Carson is also home to the 10th Special Forces Group (Airborne).

### Do most Fort Carson families live on post?

No. Per the Fort Carson Fact Sheet, 82% of Soldiers and families live off post.

### What is the BAH for an E-5 at Fort Carson?

In 2026, an E-5 with dependents gets $2,358 a month in the Colorado Springs rate area (CO046). Without dependents, the rate is $1,860.

### Is Pueblo Chemical Depot still open?

No. The depot finished destroying its chemical weapons in June 2023 and was deactivated in September 2024.
